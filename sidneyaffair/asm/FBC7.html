FBC7: Sound: Noise
Makes a short noise on the speaker by toggling bit 4 of port FE for 0080 pulses. Called by BASIC as "noi" (FBC7).
Sound_Noise FBC7 DI Disable interrupts.
FBC8 LD BC,$0080
FBCB LD A,($5C48)
FBCE RRCA
FBCF RRCA
FBD0 RRCA
FBD1 EX AF,AF'
FBD2 LD H,$00
Sound_Noise_0 FBD4 EX AF,AF'
FBD5 OUT ($FE),A
FBD7 XOR $10
FBD9 EX AF,AF'
FBDA LD L,$00
FBDC LD D,L
FBDD LD E,H
FBDE AND A
FBDF SBC HL,DE
FBE1 SBC HL,DE
FBE3 LD DE,$00FE
FBE6 ADD HL,DE
FBE7 LD A,L
FBE8 SUB H
FBE9 JR C,Sound_Noise_1
FBEB DEC A
Sound_Noise_1 FBEC LD H,A
Sound_Noise_2 FBED DEC A
FBEE JR NZ,Sound_Noise_2
FBF0 DEC BC
FBF1 LD A,B
FBF2 OR C
FBF3 JR NZ,Sound_Noise_0
FBF5 RET
