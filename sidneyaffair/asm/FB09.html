FB09: Display Frame
Copies one frame of the picture held in the buffer at PictureBuffer to the screen: first the pixel rows, then the attributes from PictureBuffer_Attributes. Called by BASIC as "ins" (FB09) with the frame number POKEd into DisplayFrame_Number.
The routine works out the frame's row and column in the sheet from *DisplayFrame_Number and *DisplayFrame_PerRow, converts those to byte offsets with DisplayFrame_CalculateOffset, and copies *DisplayFrame_Height character rows of *DisplayFrame_LineWidth bytes from the buffer to the screen at *DisplayFrame_Column / *DisplayFrame_Row. It then copies the matching attributes from the buffer (offset by 1800) to the attribute file.
The values in the snapshot describe rows of 04 frames of 08 by 0A characters, copied to column 19 row 03 after line 60.
