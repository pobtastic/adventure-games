64708: Print String: Print Character
Used by the routine at PrintString.
Builds two 4-pixel characters side by side in the UDG for "U" (graphic code 164) from the font at Font, and prints that UDG after every second character. The flag at PrintString_HalfFlag records whether a half is waiting.
