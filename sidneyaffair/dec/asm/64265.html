64265: Display Frame
Copies one frame of the picture held in the buffer at PictureBuffer to the screen: first the pixel rows, then the attributes from PictureBuffer_Attributes. Called by BASIC as "ins" (64265) with the frame number POKEd into DisplayFrame_Number.
The routine works out the frame's row and column in the sheet from *DisplayFrame_Number and *DisplayFrame_PerRow, converts those to byte offsets with DisplayFrame_CalculateOffset, and copies *DisplayFrame_Height character rows of *DisplayFrame_LineWidth bytes from the buffer to the screen at *DisplayFrame_Column / *DisplayFrame_Row. It then copies the matching attributes from the buffer (offset by 6144) to the attribute file.
The values in the snapshot describe rows of 4 frames of 8 by 10 characters, copied to column 25 row 3 after line 60.
