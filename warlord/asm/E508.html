E508: Table: Scenic Event Items
One item ID per scenic event. 00 means no item is needed. Table_ItemDescriptions has no description for these IDs, so there is no item name for them. They are scenic-event slots in Table_ItemEventLocations. Six of them are also listed in Table_ItemGrouping, whose nouns are in Table_ObjectNounPhrases: 02 and 03 in Data_ItemGroup_Roman (Roman), 04 and 05 in Data_ItemGroup_Fomorian (Fomorian) and Data_ItemGroup_Warrior (Warrior), 06 in Data_ItemGroup_Boar (Boar), and 09 in Data_ItemGroup_Rats (Rats). The remaining IDs are not in those tables. GameScenicEventChecker passes the byte to ItemLocator to find the room that slot currently occupies. See Count_ScenicEvents for the count.
