704F: Graphics: The Shrine Of The Nemed
The Shrine Of The Nemed
Mask bytes. Each bit stands for one screen byte. When the bit is set, Print_RoomImage writes a pixel byte from Image_NemedShrine_Pixels; when it is clear, DE still advances but nothing is written, so those 8 pixels stay blank. Print_RoomImage starts here and skips ahead 0200 bytes to the pixel data.
