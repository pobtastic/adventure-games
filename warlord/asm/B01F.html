B01F: Move Player To Room
Used by the routine at ChangeRoom.
Moves the player to a new room. Before updating the current room, it brings every scenic-event item that is here or carried along to the destination. It then moves each item whose bit is set in Flag_EventStates into the new room (see Table_TurnBasedEventItems).
Input
A Destination room ID
