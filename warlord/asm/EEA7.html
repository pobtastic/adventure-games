EEA7: Process: Examine Body
Are any of the variations of the body in the current room/ players inventory?
Process_ExamineBody EEA7 LD HL,$F88F Call CheckItemGroupPresent with Data_ItemGroup_Body_Duplicate.
EEAA CALL CheckItemGroupPresent
Print "You see nothing special." if the body is not present in the current room.
EEAD JP NZ,Response_YouSeeNothingSpecial Jump to Response_YouSeeNothingSpecial if the body is not present in the current room.
Print "The body is very emaciated.The face,though drawn,reflects deep tranquility."
EEB0 LD HL,$D1B3 HL=Messaging_BodyVeryEmaciated.
EEB3 JP PrintStringAndNewline_Duplicate Jump to PrintStringAndNewline_Duplicate.
