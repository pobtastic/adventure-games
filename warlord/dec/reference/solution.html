Overview
Complete the quest through the Celtic otherworld and return via the ring of crystals.
Primary walkthrough: Jacob Gunness (May 1999) via the Classic Adventures Solution Archive (https://solutionarchive.com/file/id,825/), with thanks recorded there to Zeljko Juric for the ending.
  • Wear Lug's helmet for the causeway warrior
  • Wear the white cloak for fire walls
  • Wear the amulet in the Vale of Whispers
  • Silver is unimportant; food only if energy runs low (Gunness)

6. Capture The Roman
After scoring about 50%, walk around until a Roman attacks.
Command Notes
KILL ROMAN He flees when attacked
CAPTURE ROMAN Use the rope once you find him again in the southern area
Then go to the Circle of Stones (from Cadwell Hillfort: E, E, N) for the druid exchange below.
Gunness thanks Zeljko Juric for help with the ending.
