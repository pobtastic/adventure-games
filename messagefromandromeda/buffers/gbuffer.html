Table: Noun Tokens
The vocabulary number (Table_Vocabulary) of each of the 25 nouns the player can name (the count is at Count_Nouns). The position of a word in this list is its object number, which also indexes Table_ObjectNounPhrases for its name in "I can't see ... here." replies.
Reached through Pointer_NounTokens: Parser_FindPhrase counts the object words in a command, and GameLoop_Step3 looks each named noun up here before calling Parser_ResolvePhrase.

Table: Item Groups
For each object in Table_ObjectNounPhrases, in the same order: the items it may refer to, ending with FE. Parser_ResolvePhrase skips to this noun's group from *Pointer_ItemGroupingTable.
891C 36
Table: Verb Word Tokens
The vocabulary number (Table_Vocabulary) of each of the 24 verbs (the count is at Count_VerbTokens). Handler_DispatchVerb looks the first word of a command up here and jumps to the action at the same position in JumpTable_Verbs.
8940 538
Table: Phrase Tokens
The words an action expects after its verb, as vocabulary numbers (Table_Vocabulary). FD separates alternative wordings and FE ends a phrase. An action points HL at a phrase and calls Parser_AdvanceToSeparator. That compares it with the words after the verb and sets the zero flag when one of the wordings matches the whole of the rest of the command.
8B5A 384
Table: Room Map
Six bytes per room, in the order north, south, east, west, up, down. A zero means there is no exit.
8CFC 26
Table: Rooms With Images
The 1A rooms that have a picture (the count is at Count_RoomsWithImages). Handler_DisplayRoom looks the current room up here (through Pointer_RoomsWithImages); where it is found picks the drawing routine in JumpTable_RoomImages and the "already seen" bit in Table_RoomImagesAlreadySeen. A picture is shown the first time the player enters the room, and again whenever they type LOOK.
