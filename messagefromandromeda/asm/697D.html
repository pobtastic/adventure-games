697D: Parser: Validate Direct Object
Used by the routines at ActionExamine, ActionTake, ActionDrop, ActionEnter, ActionGive, ActionCut, ActionKill, ActionRead, ActionOpen, ActionClose, ActionShoot, ActionWear, ActionDrink, ActionTurn, ActionTouch, ResponseYouCanT2, ResponseItHasNoApparentEffect and ActionUse.
In most adventure games, the structure for a command is "verb + direct object". The verb describes the action, and the direct object is what the action is performed on. For example; "TAKE KEY" uses the verb "TAKE" on the direct object "KEY".
