9861: Action: Wear
Wear the named item of clothing.

9864 RET C
9865 JR ActionWear_Step1 Continue at ActionWear_Step1 to match what the player wants to wear.
ActionWear_Loop1 9867 LD HL,$645B Print "You can't." and start a new line.
ActionWear_Loop2 986A CALL PrintStringAndANewline
986D RET Return.
ActionWear_Step1 986E LD HL,$897D Jump to ActionWear_Step4 unless the command matches the phrase at 897D (A pair of gloves,which you are wearing. (39)).
9871 CALL Parser_AdvanceToSeparator

9876 LD A,$39
9878 CALL IsObjectCarried
987B JR NZ,ActionWear_Step2
987D LD HL,$7F26 Print "You're already wearing them." and start a new line.

9899 CALL UpdateObjectLocation
989C LD BC,$3901 Move A pair of gloves,which you are wearing. (39) to the inventory (01).
989F CALL UpdateObjectLocation
98A2 LD HL,$6466 Print "O.K." and start a new line.
98A5 JP ActionWear_Loop2
ActionWear_Step4 98A8 JP ActionWear_Loop1 Continue at ActionWear_Loop1 to say the player can't.
