8CFC: Table: Rooms With Images
The 1A rooms that have a picture (the count is at Count_RoomsWithImages). Handler_DisplayRoom looks the current room up here (through Pointer_RoomsWithImages); where it is found picks the drawing routine in JumpTable_RoomImages and the "already seen" bit in Table_RoomImagesAlreadySeen. A picture is shown the first time the player enters the room, and again whenever they type LOOK.
