8DB9: Handler: Ask To Play Again
Used by the routines at GameComplete and ActionQuit.
Read Y or N, then reset the machine or start another game.
HandlerAskToPlayAgain 8DB9 LD HL,$639A Print Want another game? Y/N..
8DBC CALL PrintStringAndANewline
HandlerAskToPlayAgain_Loop1 8DBF CALL GetUserInput Call GetUserInput to wait for a keypress.
8DC2 CP $4E Reset the computer when N (4E) was pressed.
8DC4 JP Z,$0000
8DC7 CP $59 Jump back to GameEntryPoint when Y (59) was pressed.
8DC9 JP Z,GameEntryPoint
8DCC JR HandlerAskToPlayAgain_Loop1 Continue at HandlerAskToPlayAgain_Loop1 until the player presses Y or N.
