8E0C: Handler: Complex Explodes
Destroy the player when the alien complex explodes.
HandlerComplexExplodes 8E0C LD HL,$8456 Point HL at "The complex explodes. You are caught in the explosion." and continue at Handler_PlayerKilled to print it and end the game.
