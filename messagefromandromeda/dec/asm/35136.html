The words an action expects after its verb, as vocabulary numbers (Table_Vocabulary). 253 separates alternative wordings and 254 ends a phrase. An action points HL at a phrase and calls Parser_AdvanceToSeparator. That compares it with the words after the verb and sets the zero flag when one of the wordings matches the whole of the rest of the command.
