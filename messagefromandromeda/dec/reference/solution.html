Overview
Land on the planet, recover what you need from the alien complex, and take off again in your ship.
Source: Classic Adventures Solution Archive / Tipshop complete solution (CASA game page https://solutionarchive.com/game/id,353/ credits Dorothy; the .rtf solution file requires login).
  • Wear gloves before handling the fungus
  • Published tips cover the rod/plate and sphere puzzles
Published Tips
Crash/YS tips mirrored on Tipshop: POINT ROD AT PLATE to open the panel; move and rotate the sphere between pedestals/dish; READ MESSAGE / LAND / OPEN AIRLOCK / LEAVE to begin. Cut ropes for the skeleton / missile chain; wear gloves to get fungus for the slug.
