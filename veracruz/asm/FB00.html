FB00: Display Frame
Copies one frame of the picture held in the buffer at PictureBuffer to the screen: first the pixel rows, then the attributes from PictureBuffer_Attributes. Called by BASIC with the frame number POKEd into DisplayFrame_Number.
The values in the snapshot describe rows of 03 frames of 0A by 11 characters, copied to column 03 row 02.
DisplayFrame FB00 JR DisplayFrame_Copy Jump to DisplayFrame_Copy.
