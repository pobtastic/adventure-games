Table: Item Locations
Where each of the 4F items is (the count is at Count_Items). The item number is the position in this table, and the same number indexes the item descriptions at Table_ItemDescriptions.
Byte Meaning
00 Not in play yet, or used up
01 Carried by the player
Other The room the item is in
This is the start of the game status buffer. GameStart copies the 0144 bytes from DefaultGameState over it at the start of every game (that list names every item and where it starts), and SaveGame and LoadTape save and load it as a single tape block.
Populated by GameStart and Handler_UpdateObjectLocation. See PrintObjects, ValidateItemPresent, ObjectEventLocator and CheckRoomObjects for usage.

Turn-Based Event Counters
BC6F 1
Flag: Crab Following
Only bit 0 is used. When it is set, the giant crab ("A giant crab", the first byte of Table_FollowingItems) follows the player from room to room. ChangeRoom sets the bit at EF91 when the player walks into The Rockpool while the crab is there, and clears it at EF64 on reaching The Rocky Path. Action_Shout clears it at Shout_Crab when the boulder falls on the crab. Bits 1-7 are never set. MovePlayerToRoom moves the item for each set bit into the room the player just entered.
BC70 8
Table: Following Items
Each bit of Flag_CrabFollowing makes the item at the same position here follow the player: every time the player changes room, MovePlayerToRoom moves the item into the new room. Only bit 0 is used. ChangeRoom sets it when the player walks into The Rockpool while the giant crab is there, and closes the way out to the west and north; the crab then chases the player until they reach The Rocky Path or until the boulder falls on it (Action_Shout).
BC78 9
Table: Scenic Event Locations
Where each of the 09 scenic events is (the count is at Count_ScenicEvents). Scenic events are the animals and pirates that wander about; in the item groups at Data_ItemGroup_Match event 00 is written as 80 and so on.
Byte Meaning
00 Not in play
FF Just put into play by Handler_PutScenicEventIntoPlay, so it gets a room on the next turn
Other The room the event is in
Each turn GameEventsProcessor moves every event that is in play, and not with the player, to a random room from its list in Data_ScenicEvents_Seagull, found through the pointers at Table_ScenicEventRooms. When the player leaves a room MovePlayerToRoom takes any event that is there along too, and Handler_ScenicEvents runs the handler from JumpTable_ScenicEvents for every event in the player's room.
Populated by GameStart and Handler_UpdateObjectLocation. See GameEventsProcessor, ValidateItemPresent, ObjectEventLocator, Handler_ScenicEvents and MovePlayerToRoom for usage.

BD66 10
User Input: Word Tokens
The vocabulary number (Table_Vocabulary) of each word of the last command, in the order they were typed, with FF in every slot that isn't used. "THE" is skipped, and a word that isn't in the vocabulary stops the command with an error, so the words always fill the slots from the start.
Populated by UserInput_Enter, which first fills all 0A slots with FF. Handler_MatchVerb looks the verb up in Table_VerbWordTokens, MatchPhraseTokens matches the words after it against a phrase from PhraseTokens_Bottle, and Parser_CountItems and Parser_ProcessItem look through every slot for the name of an object. See also Parser_ValidateNoDirectObject, Parser_ValidateDirectObject, Parser_ValidateInputTokens, Parser_ValidateAnyDirectObject and Action_DropThrow for usage.
