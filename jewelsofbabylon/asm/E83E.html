E83E: Table: Object Words
The vocabulary number (Table_Vocabulary) of each of the 30 objects the player can name (the count is at Count_Objects). The position of a word in this list is its object number, which also indexes Table_ItemGrouping for the items it may mean and Table_ObjectNounPhrases for its name in "I can't see ... here." replies.
Reached through Pointer_ObjectList: Parser_CountItems counts the object words in a command, and Parser_ProcessItem finds the first one and passes it to Parser_CheckObjectIsHere.
