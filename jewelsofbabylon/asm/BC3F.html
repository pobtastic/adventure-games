BC3F: Unused Item Locations
Spare bytes in the 64-byte item-location table at Table_ItemLocations. Count_Items is 4F, and no instruction addresses these 15 bytes. GameStart, SaveGame and LoadTape still copy them with the rest of the 0144-byte game state.
