48191: Unused Item Locations
Spare bytes in the 100-byte item-location table at Table_ItemLocations. Count_Items is 79, and no instruction addresses these 21 bytes. GameStart, SaveGame and LoadTape still copy them with the rest of the 324-byte game state.
