59850: Data: Phrase Tokens
The words an action expects after its verb, as vocabulary numbers (Table_Vocabulary). 253 separates alternative wordings and 254 ends a phrase; 255 at the start of a wording means "nothing at all". An action points HL at a phrase and calls MatchPhraseTokens. That compares it with the words after the verb (UserInput_Token_2) and sets the zero flag when one of the wordings matches the whole of the rest of the command.
