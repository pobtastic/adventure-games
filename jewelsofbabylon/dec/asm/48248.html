48248: Table: Scenic Event Locations
Where each of the 9 scenic events is (the count is at Count_ScenicEvents). Scenic events are the animals and pirates that wander about; in the item groups at Data_ItemGroup_Match event 0 is written as 128 and so on.
Byte Meaning
0 Not in play
255 Just put into play by Handler_PutScenicEventIntoPlay, so it gets a room on the next turn
Other The room the event is in
Each turn GameEventsProcessor moves every event that is in play, and not with the player, to a random room from its list in Data_ScenicEvents_Seagull, found through the pointers at Table_ScenicEventRooms. When the player leaves a room MovePlayerToRoom takes any event that is there along too, and Handler_ScenicEvents runs the handler from JumpTable_ScenicEvents for every event in the player's room.
Populated by GameStart and Handler_UpdateObjectLocation. See GameEventsProcessor, ValidateItemPresent, ObjectEventLocator, Handler_ScenicEvents and MovePlayerToRoom for usage.
