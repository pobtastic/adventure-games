Overview
Recover the Jewels of Babylon from the island and return them to your ship.
Primary walkthrough: Jacob Gunness (1993) via the Classic Adventures Solution Archive (file id 401).
  • Save often - pirates can kill you
  • The map is full of useless red-herring objects
Hazards And Red Herrings
Gunness: save regularly; nasty pirates may kill you. The game is flooded with useless objects - ignore extras that are not on this route. Tips also note giving the watch to the native, fish to the lion, killing the octopus with the spear, crowbar for the boulder/stone, rod for the rock door, and shouting in the canyon for the crab.
