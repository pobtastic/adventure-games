62402: Drawing
Used by the routine at ProcessTextToken.
Draws one location picture from line, fill and attribute commands. Pictures are not bitmaps. The pen starts at X=127 and Y=63 (Y counts up from the bottom, so this is screen row 112). 0 ends the picture. 8 followed by two bytes moves the pen. Bit 7 draws a line, bit 6 fills, and bit 5 paints attributes.
