46273: Find The First Noun
Used by the routines at InitialiseDisplayBuffer and ReadDisplaySetupFlag.
Finds the first noun. A value of 5 at DisplayMode takes the noun already prepared in the flag bytes. Otherwise, when bit 7 of DisplayModeFlag is clear, it scans objects from ObjectTablePointer with the handler at ProcessObjectEntryAlternate or ProcessObjectEntry.
