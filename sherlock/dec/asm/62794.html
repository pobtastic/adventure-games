62794: Step Pen Up
Used by the routines at DrawingFill and DrawingLine.
Moves the pen one pixel up the screen. E counts up from the bottom row, because PixelAddress hands it to the ROM PIXEL-ADD routine. When the pen would pass the top row (E=176) it stays put and the zero flag is set.
StepPenUp 62794 INC E Move the pen up and return when it is still on screen.

62796 LD A,E
62797 CP 176
62799 LD A,H
62800 RET NZ
62801 DEC E The pen passed the top row, so step back and tell the caller it did not move.
