50354: Check Display Flag Bits 2 And 6
Used by the routines at ProcessObject_Display, InitialiseDisplayBuffer and ProcessDisplayAction.
Tests bits 2 and 6 of the object attribute at ObjectAttributeValue. When both allow it, the display flag chooses the mode.
