44283: Apply The Saved Clause
Used by the routine at StartPictureModeScreenIdle.
When the command mode at 40996 is 2 and bit 6 of E is clear, copies six bytes from offset 8 of the clause record to offset 24 and returns to the scan at ReadObjectProcessingFlag. Once the game has started, any other case sets bit 3 of E and returns to that scan. Before the game has started it restores the saved word as class 176 unlinks the clause record, and falls into MatchWordAgainstEarlierClause.
