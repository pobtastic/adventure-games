C306: Scroll Screen And Reset Display
Used by the routines at PrintMessageAndInitialiseTimer and ProcessScreenCharacter.
Waits for a key, then resets the screen display.
ScrollScreenAndResetDisplay C306 PUSH HL Call WaitForKeypress_Loop to wait for keypress.
C307 LD HL,$5A5F
C30A SET 7,(HL)
C30C CALL WaitForKeypress_Loop
C30F RES 7,(HL) Clear bit 7, call ResetScreenDisplay to reset the screen.
C311 CALL ResetScreenDisplay
C314 POP HL Restore HL from the stack.
C315 RET Return.
