AF38: Copy Schedule Entry
Used by the routine at MergeCharacterFlags.
Copies the ten-byte schedule entry into the character record the caller is building.

AF39 PUSH BC
AF3A PUSH HL
AF3B LD B,$0A Take a count of 0A.

AF3F POP HL
AF40 LD E,A

AF49 POP HL Restore the pointer, the count and the source from the stack.
