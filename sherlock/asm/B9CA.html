B9CA: Copy The Link Ahead Of This Record
Used by the routine at ProcessScriptDisplay.
Reads the saved character record and copies the link ahead of it into the object attribute.
CopyLinkAhead B9CA LD IX,($B866) Read the saved character record.
CopyLinkAhead_StoreLink B9CE LD L,(IY-$02) Read the link word stored before the record at IY.
B9D1 LD H,(IY-$01)
B9D4 LD (IX+$05),L Store the low byte of the link at offset 05.
B9D7 LD (IX+$06),H Store the high byte of the link at offset 06 and keep IX.
B9DA PUSH IX
B9DC PUSH IY Copy the memory-link record from IY into IX.
B9DE POP IX
B9E0 CALL ProcessMemoryEntry Call ProcessMemoryEntry to follow this memory link.
B9E3 POP IX
B9E5 LD A,L Jump ahead to CopyLinkAhead_ClearAndReturn if the pointer is 0000.
B9E6 OR H
B9E7 JR Z,CopyLinkAhead_ClearAndReturn
B9E9 PUSH HL Point IY at HL and jump to CopyLinkAhead_StoreLink if bit 6 of IY+01 is set.
B9EA POP IY
B9EC BIT 6,(IY+$01)
B9F0 JR NZ,CopyLinkAhead_StoreLink
B9F2 OR %00000001 Reset the zero flag.
B9F4 RET Return.
Copies the pointer and clears the result.
