F105: Set Bit 1 Of The Indexed Character
Sets bit 1 of the character record at 996F and reports success.
Run by CA64 after the display record at F103, from the show-message command at F0E6.

ProcessCharacterAttribute_ActionSucceeded F10D OR %00000001 Reset the zero flag.
