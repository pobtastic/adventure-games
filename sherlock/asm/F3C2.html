F3C2: Drawing
Used by the routine at ProcessTextToken.
Draws one location picture from line, fill and attribute commands. Pictures are not bitmaps. The pen starts at X=7F and Y=3F (Y counts up from the bottom, so this is screen row 112). 00 ends the picture. 08 followed by two bytes moves the pen. Bit 7 draws a line, bit 6 fills, and bit 5 paints attributes.
