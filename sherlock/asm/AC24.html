AC24: Clear Co-ordinate Buffer
Used by the routines at StartPictureModeScreenIdle and CopyDisplayIfNeeded.
Clears the co-ordinate buffer and marks bits 2, 4 and 5 of E.
ClearCoordinateBuffer AC24 LD HL,$AAA8 Call ClearBuffer to clear buffer.
AC27 LD B,$0A
AC29 CALL ClearBuffer
AC2C LD A,E Read the low byte of the source.
AC2D OR $34 Mark bits 2, 4, 5 on the result.
AC2F LD E,A Store the result in the low byte of the source.
Saves the text pointer, and a code of 128 clears the fourth flag bit.
ClearCoordinateBuffer_Loop AC30 CALL SaveTextPointer Go to ClearCoordinateBuffer_TestLowAndHighToken if it is not 80.
AC33 CP $80
AC35 JR NZ,ClearCoordinateBuffer_TestLowAndHighToken
AC37 BIT 4,E Jump ahead to ClearCoordinateBuffer_Next if bit 4 is clear.
AC39 JR Z,ClearCoordinateBuffer_Next
AC3B RES 4,E Clear the mark on this character.
AC3D JR ClearCoordinateBuffer_Loop
The condition holds, so the caller continues.
ClearCoordinateBuffer_Next AC3F SCF Mark the condition as met.
AC40 RET Return.
Accepts token types 10 and 70 when their flag bits are set.
