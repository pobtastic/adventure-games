F5FB: Pixel Address
Used by the routines at KeepOnlyBitsThatAreSet and PlotPixel.
Asks the ROM for the display address of this pixel and builds the bit mask for it. E is counted up from the bottom of the screen, and the ROM turns that into the screen row.
PixelAddress F5FB PUSH BC Call PIXEL_ADD.
F5FC LD B,E
F5FD LD C,D
F5FE CALL $22AA
F601 LD B,A Take the column the ROM returned, count one further, and start the mask at 01.
F602 INC B
F603 LD A,$01
PixelAddress_Loop F605 RRC A Shift the pixel mask until it covers this column.
F607 DJNZ PixelAddress_Loop
F609 POP BC
F60A RET Return.
