CE8F: Continue Script Execution
Used by the routines at DispatchCharacterScript, PrintCommandMessage, ShowRoomDescription, CDBF, CE18, ReadCharactersRoom and RunSimpleAction.
Resumes the character script after a command returns, and follows the script pointer when the record flag says to.
