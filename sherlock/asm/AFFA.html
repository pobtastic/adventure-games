AFFA: Remember The Display Page
Remembers display page A0 and joins the routine that saves the display state. Reached through the alternate command table at Table_CommandHandlerLookup_Alternate, dispatched by AFE0.
RememberDisplayPage AFFA LD D,$A0 Take token A0 and continue at HandleClassAWord.
