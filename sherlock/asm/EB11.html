EB11: Display Record
Display record read by ProcessDisplayRecord. The show-message commands at EA31 and EA43 pass this word to ProcessDisplayRecordFromScript.
Named from the script commands at EA31 and EA43.
EB11 DEFB $00,$00 Display record read by ProcessDisplayRecord: flags 00 value 00.
