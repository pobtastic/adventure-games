CB28: Saved Index Registers
CB34 saves IX here and IY at CB2A. RunDisplayRecordCode brings both back before the next display record runs. Used by RunDisplayRecordCode and CB34.
CB28 DEFW $0000
CB2A DEFW $0000 Read by RunDisplayRecordCode and CB34.
