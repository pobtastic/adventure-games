EB1B: Point At The Other Character
Points at the character record at 9997 and tests bit 1 of its action flags. A non-zero return means the script step succeeded.
Run by CA64 after the display record at EB19, from the show-message command at EA2C.
CopyCharacterOntoBothRecords_OtherRecord EB1B LD IX,$9997 Point at the character record at 9997.
This entry point is used by the routine at CopyCharacterOntoBothRecords_ActionFlags.
CopyCharacterOntoBothRecords_TestActionBit EB1F LD HL,$0000 Clear the pointer and the carry.
EB22 AND A
EB23 BIT 1,(IX+$03) See whether bit 1 of the action flags is set.
EB27 RET Return.
