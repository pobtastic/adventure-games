A35D: Saved Object Fields
Scratch copy of an object id and two room bytes. ProcessObjectDisplay stores the id from A033 and the room word from RoomDisplayParameter. CDBF stores three record bytes here and copies them back. Used by ProcessObjectDisplay, ProcessObject_Display and CDBF.
A35D DEFB $00 Object id.
A35E DEFB $00 Room byte, or the low byte of the room word from RoomDisplayParameter.
A35F DEFB $00 Second room byte, or the high byte of that word.
