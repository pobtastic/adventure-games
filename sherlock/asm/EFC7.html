EFC7: Display Record
Display record read by ProcessDisplayRecord. The show-message commands at EE46, EEC3, EEDB and EF09 pass this word to ProcessDisplayRecordFromScript.
Named from the script commands at EE46, EEC3, EEDB and EF09.
