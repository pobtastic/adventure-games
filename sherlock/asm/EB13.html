EB13: Point At The First Character
Points at the character record at 99A9 and joins the flag test.
Run by CA64 after the display record at EB11, from the show-message commands at EA31 and EA43.
CopyCharacterOntoBothRecords_ActionFlags EB13 LD IX,$99A9 Point at the character record at 99A9.
EB17 JR CopyCharacterOntoBothRecords_TestActionBit Go to CopyCharacterOntoBothRecords_TestActionBit to test bit 1 of the action flags.
