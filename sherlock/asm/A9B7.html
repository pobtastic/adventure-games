A9B7: Print Character
Used by the routines at PrintNewline, PrintFullStop, ShiftTextPointer, PrintHyphen, PrintLetterD, PrintOneDecimalDigit, PrintHundredsTensAndUnits, TestSpace, PrintThisCharacter, LeaveFullStop, PrintTheControlCharacter, PrintTextToken, PrintTextBasedOnFlag, PrintLocationText, PrintFormattedObjectList, ParseInput, ReadCommandLine_CommandLinePrints, LeaveQuoteResult, TestFullStop, MapCursorKeys, IgnoreInputUntilGameHasStarted, DeleteInputCharacter, PrintObjectDescriptionText and ProcessCharacterAttribute.
In word-wrap mode, stores each letter at *BufferPointer in the word buffer at WordBuffer until a space, newline or quote, then prints the buffered word. When the print mode flag at PrintModeFlag is set, uses the alternate printer instead.
