D607: Read The Room Graphics Link
Reads the room-graphics link stored at the pointer. Reached from the script records at E182 and E1AC.
D607 LD E,(HL) Read the low byte of the room-graphics link.
D608 INC HL Advance through this record.
D609 LD D,(HL) Read the high byte of the room-graphics link.
D60A RET Return.
