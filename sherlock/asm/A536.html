A536: Wait For A Key With Timeout
Used by the routine at ReadCommandLine_CommandLinePrints.
Waits for a key until the timer at A039+02 runs out. GetKeypress sets bit 7 when a key is pressed. When the timer expires, DeleteInputCharacter runs. Either way the timer is reset to 0BB8.
