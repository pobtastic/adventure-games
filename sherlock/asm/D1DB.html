D1DB: Jump To Handler
Used by the routines at RecordResultCharacterScript and CheckBitAndProcess.
Jumps to the handler address just looked up. Callers leave that address in the pointer.
JumpToHandler D1DB JP (HL) Jump to the handler whose address the caller left in HL.
