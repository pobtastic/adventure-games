A63F: Choose The Line Break
Puts 60 in D and jumps to LeaveFullStop, which skips the full stop but still prints a new line. ChooseTheLineBreak_PrintStop puts 30 in D, which makes LeaveFullStop print a full stop and a new line.
Control code 1D in the table at JumpTable_ControlCharacters.
Used by the routine at DispatchTextControlHandler.
