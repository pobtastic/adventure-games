E6EA: Store The Action Flags
Copies the room display parameter onto the character and reports success.
Run by CA64 after the display record at E6E6, from the show-message commands at F2F7, F35F and F371.
StoreActionFlags E6EA CALL FindCharacterScriptEntry Call FindCharacterScriptEntry to find this character's script entry.
E6ED LD A,($A034) Copy the room display parameter into the character's action flags.
E6F0 LD (IX+$03),A
E6F3 OR %00000001 Reset the zero flag.
E6F5 RET Return.
