EFAE: Print This Handler's Message
Prints the message at 75D2+31.
Run by CA64 after the display record at EFAC, from the show-message command at EF01.
PrintHandlerMessage EFAE LD HL,$7603 Take the message at 75D2+31 and go to SetPrintFlagAndPrint_0 to print it.
EFB1 JR SetPrintFlagAndPrint_0
