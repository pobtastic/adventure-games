A2AB: Clear Buffer
Used by the routines at ClearCoordinateBuffer, InitialiseDisplayBuffer, ProcessDisplayPointer, ProcessLocationDisplay and ParseInput.
Blanks the buffer before the next step uses it.
ClearBuffer A2AB XOR A Start from zero.
ClearBuffer_0 A2AC LD (HL),A Count down and repeat from ClearBuffer_0 until the count runs out.
A2AD INC HL
A2AE DJNZ ClearBuffer_0
A2B0 RET Return.
