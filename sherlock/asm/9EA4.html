9EA4: Test The Action-Taken Flag
Prints the character-busy message unless the action-taken flag is set.
Run by CA64 after the display record at 9EA2, from the show-message command at EAB3.
TestActionTakenFlag 9EA4 LD HL,$6A33 Take the character busy message.
