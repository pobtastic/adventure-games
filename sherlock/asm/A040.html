A040: Game Entry Point
Used by the routines at Alias_GameEntryPoint and A0EB.
Starts a game: clears the parser, asks whether pictures are wanted, then runs each character from the timetable.
GameEntryPoint A040 XOR A Clear the parser workspace and the initialised flag.
A041 LD ($D6B8),A
A044 LD ($A01B),A
A047 DI
Draw decorative line.

A04B LD SP,$5D80 Point the stack at Table_WordIndex.
A04E CALL WaitForKeypress_Loop Call WaitForKeypress_Loop to wait for keypress.
A051 CP $6E Jump to GameEntryPoint_ClearFlags if the player pressed "n".
A053 JR Z,GameEntryPoint_ClearFlags
A055 CP $4E See whether the key is N.
The player didn't press N, so pictures stay enabled.
A057 LD A,$01 Go to GameEntryPoint_Next with 01 (pictures on) unless the key was N.
A059 JR NZ,GameEntryPoint_Next
The player pressed either "N" or "n".
GameEntryPoint_ClearFlags A05B XOR A Clear the pictures-enabled flag. The player pressed n.
GameEntryPoint_Next A05C LD ($A011),A Record the pictures-enabled flag.
