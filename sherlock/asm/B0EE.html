B0EE: Calculate Location Offset
Used by the routines at LoadGameState and SaveGameState.
Subtracts the location table at Table_Locations from the room pointer and returns that distance.
Output
DE Offset from base location address
CalculateLocationOffset B0EE LD IX,$840B Use the locations at Table_Locations and the character script cycle at CharacterScriptCycles.
