BA20: Process Character Display
Used by the routine at ProcessScriptDisplay.
Reads the character being processed and clears its script list for ids 02 and 03.
ProcessCharacterDisplay BA20 LD A,($A036) Read the character being processed and take the workspace byte at BA67.
BA23 LD HL,$BA67
BA26 CP $03 Jump ahead to ProcessCharacterDisplay_Next if the character being processed is 03.
BA28 JR Z,ProcessCharacterDisplay_Next
BA2A LD HL,$BB67 Jump ahead to ProcessCharacterDisplay_Store if the character being processed is not 02.
BA2D CP $02
BA2F JR NZ,ProcessCharacterDisplay_Store
ProcessCharacterDisplay_Next BA31 CALL RecordResultCharacterScript Call RecordResultCharacterScript to clear the character-script list.
ProcessCharacterDisplay_Store BA34 LD A,$01 Take 01.
BA36 POP HL Restore HL, IY and IX from the stack.
BA37 POP IY
BA39 POP IX
BA3B AND A Clear the carry before marking this character display as finished.
BA3C SCF Mark the condition as met.
BA3D RET Return.
