AFE1: Check Bit And Jump
Used by the routines at SaveDisplayState, ClearTheDisplayLines, WaitForAKey, ResetClock, StartFreshScriptCycle, SetDisplayMode_SelectsPictureMode and SelectPictureModeAlternate.
Returns when bit 1 of E is set. Otherwise drops the return address and jumps to CopyBytes_ReportSuccess.
CheckBitAndJump AFE1 BIT 1,E See whether bit 1 of the low byte of the source is set.
AFE3 RET NZ Return if that marker is set.
AFE4 POP HL Jump back to CopyBytes_ReportSuccess to return non-zero.
AFE5 JP CopyBytes_ReportSuccess
