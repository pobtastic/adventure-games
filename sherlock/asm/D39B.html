D39B: Process Object Entry Alternate
Walks the objects in this room and describes each one that is here. Pointed at by B4E7 and B58E.

D39D PUSH DE
D39E PUSH BC
D39F PUSH IX
D3A1 POP BC Copy the character record from IX into BC.
D3A2 LD DE,$0001 Take an offset of 0001.
