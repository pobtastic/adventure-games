C8C6: Process Object Attribute
Used by the routines at TestSeventhMarker and CombineCharacterRoom.
Looks the object up, then reads its attribute through GetObjectAttributeValue.

C8C7 PUSH AF
C8C8 LD A,B
C8C9 CALL FetchObjectDataPointer
C8CC LD A,B Take the count, then call GetObjectAttributeValue to get object attribute value.
C8CD CALL GetObjectAttributeValue

C8D3 JR NC,ProcessObjectAttribute_LookObjectRun_0
