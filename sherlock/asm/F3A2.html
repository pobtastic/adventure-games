F3A2: Script: Refresh The Room
Shows a message, refreshes the room and jumps on. Named from the script commands at F304, F31C and F339.
F3A2 DEFB $03,$88,$E5 Show the message at E588.
F3A5 DEFB $07,$06,$E5,$FB,$FD Refresh the room from the text at E506, using the current character, the low byte of the location.
F3AA DEFB $0E,$69,$F3 Jump to the script at F369.
F3AD DEFB $07,$41,$9C,$FD,$FF Refresh the room from the text at 9C41, using the low byte of the location, FF.
F3B2 DEFB $83,$54,$E6,$C1,$F3 Show the message at E654 and otherwise continue at F3C1.
F3B7 DEFB $83,$6F,$E6,$C1,$F3 Show the message at E66F and otherwise continue at F3C1.
F3BC DEFB $07,$BC,$E6,$FC,$FB Refresh the room from the text at E6BC, using the high byte of the location, the current character.
F3C1 DEFB $0B Run the nested character script.
