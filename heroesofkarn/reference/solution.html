Overview
Free the four heroes - Beren, Istar, Haldir and Khadim - then return to the cottage with them and the treasures (diamond, sapphire, pearl, emerald, ruby and jade flower).
Primary walkthrough: Jacob Gunness (1993) via the Classic Adventures Solution Archive (https://solutionarchive.com/file/id,347/), structured by hero as in the archive.
  • Spectrum verb forms as written by Gunness
  • On C64, address companions as: say to Name "command..."
Companion Commands
Many puzzles require a companion to act: BEREN GET SWORD, BEREN GET BIRD, BEREN ATTACK SERPENT WITH BIRD, ISTAR,READ SCROLL, ISTAR,WAVE WAND, ISTAR,READ MAP, HALDIR PLAY LYRE, KHADIM KILL PIRATE WITH DAGGER, and so on. YS tips (James Elliott, Tipshop) summarise creature/object solutions if a step fails.
