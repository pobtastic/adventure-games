ED3A: Take: Water
Take the bottle of water, or fill an empty bottle.
Take_Water ED3A LD A,$38 Call IsObjectInInventory with item 38: "A bottle of water.".
ED3C CALL IsObjectInInventory
ED3F LD A,$38 Call Take_IsPlayerHoldingItem if "A bottle of water." is in the inventory.
ED41 CALL Z,Take_IsPlayerHoldingItem
ED44 LD B,A Jump to Handler_CheckPlayerInventory_PickUpItem if "A bottle of water." is present.
ED45 CALL ValidateItemPresent
ED48 LD A,B
ED49 JP Z,Handler_CheckPlayerInventory_PickUpItem
ED4C LD A,$37 Call IsObjectInInventory with item 37: "A bottle.".
ED4E CALL IsObjectInInventory
ED51 JR NZ,Response_YouHaveNothingToCarryItIn Jump to Response_YouHaveNothingToCarryItIn if "A bottle." is not in the inventory.
ED53 LD BC,$3738 Call TransformItem to transform "A bottle." into "A bottle of water.".
ED56 CALL TransformItem
Print "You now have a bottle of water.".
ED59 LD HL,$D307 HL=Messaging_YouNowHaveABottleOfWater.
ED5C JP PrintResponse Jump to PrintResponse.
