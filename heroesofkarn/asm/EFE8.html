EFE8: Throw: Mirror To Anton
Throw the magic mirror to Anton.

EFEA CALL IsPlayerCarryingItem
EFED LD A,E Copy E into A.
EFEE CALL HandlerDestroyItemEvent Call HandlerDestroyItemEvent.
EFF1 LD HL,$AE9A Decrease *Count_InventoryItems by one.
EFF4 DEC (HL)
EFF5 LD BC,$7F80 Call TransformItem to transform "Anton,the Gypsy King. He says "Find me a magic mirror and I will tell your fortune."" into "Anton,the Gypsy King. He says "Cross my palm with silver."".
EFF8 CALL TransformItem
