ED8B: Take: Oil
Take the bottle of oil, or fill an empty bottle.
Take_Oil ED8B LD A,$3A Call IsObjectInInventory with item 3A: "A bottle of oil.".
ED8D CALL IsObjectInInventory
ED90 LD A,$3A Call Take_IsPlayerHoldingItem if "A bottle of oil." is in the inventory.
ED92 CALL Z,Take_IsPlayerHoldingItem
ED95 LD B,A Jump to Handler_CheckPlayerInventory_PickUpItem if "A bottle of oil." is present.
ED96 CALL ValidateItemPresent
ED99 LD A,B
ED9A JP Z,Handler_CheckPlayerInventory_PickUpItem
ED9D LD A,$37 Call IsObjectInInventory with item 37: "A bottle.".
ED9F CALL IsObjectInInventory
EDA2 JP NZ,Response_YouHaveNothingToCarryItIn Jump to Response_YouHaveNothingToCarryItIn if "A bottle." is not in the inventory.
EDA5 LD BC,$373A Call TransformItem to transform "A bottle." into "A bottle of oil.".
EDA8 CALL TransformItem
Print "You now have a bottle of oil.".
EDAB LD HL,$D367 HL=Messaging_YouNowHaveABottleOfOil.
EDAE JP PrintResponse Jump to PrintResponse.
