ABF1: Clear Screen
Used by the routines at HandlerDisplayRoomImage, HandlerDisplayRoomExits, GameComplete and Action_Inventory.
Clear the display before a new room or a full-screen message.
ClearScreen ABF1 CALL SwitchToNormalScreenOutput Call SwitchToNormalScreenOutput to switch to the normal screen output.
ABF4 CALL $0D6B Clear the display with CLS.
ABF7 RET Return.
