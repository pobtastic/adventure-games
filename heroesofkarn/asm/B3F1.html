B3F1: Get Table Entry
Used by the routines at ProcessGameEvents, HandlerDisplayRoomImage, PrintRoomDescription, ActionExamineItem, ValidateObject and HandlerMatchVerb.
Look up one entry in a word table.
Input
E Index of item
IX Base table address
Output
HL Address from the relevant table entry
IX Address of the table entry
