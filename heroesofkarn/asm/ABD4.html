ABD4: Game Entry Point
Used by the routine at HandlerAskToPlayAgain.
Turn caps lock on, set the screen line, and enter the cold start.
GameEntryPoint ABD4 LD HL,$5C6A Point HL at *FLAGS2.
ABD7 SET 3,(HL) Turn caps lock on.
ABD9 LD A,$18 Set the screen line to 18.
ABDB LD ($AF38),A
ABDE JP GameColdStart Continue at GameColdStart.
