ABF8: Clear Lines
Clear the lines given by *LineNumber from the bottom of the screen. Nothing calls this routine.
ClearLines ABF8 CALL SwitchToNormalScreenOutput Call SwitchToNormalScreenOutput to switch to the normal screen output.
ABFB LD A,($AF38) Clear the number of lines held by *LineNumber from the bottom of the screen using CL_LINE.
ABFE LD B,A
ABFF CALL $0E44
AC02 RET Return.
