E9E1: Change Room
Used by the routines at Handler_ShowNewRoom, MovePlayer, Action_Pour and GameStart.
Commits a move into the destination room held in A. It handles the Hall of the Phoenix and the ruined cottage, calls MovePlayerToRoom to update the current room, then jumps to Handler_ShowNewRoom to describe the new room.
Input
A Destination room ID
Entering the Hall of the Phoenix is fatal. Bit 0 of *Flags_RoomChange, when set, means the firegate does not incinerate the player.
