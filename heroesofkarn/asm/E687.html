E687: Table: Phrase Tokens
The words an action expects after its verb, as vocabulary numbers (Table_Vocabulary). FD separates alternative wordings and FE ends a phrase. An action points HL at a phrase and calls MatchPhraseTokens. That compares it with the words after the verb and sets the zero flag when one of the wordings matches the whole of the rest of the command.
