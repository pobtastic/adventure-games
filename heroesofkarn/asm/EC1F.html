EC1F: Take: Key
Take the key that is here, or ask the player to be more specific.
Take_Key EC1F LD HL,$E51D HL=Data_ItemGroup_Key.
EC22 CALL FindActiveScenicEvent Call FindActiveScenicEvent.
EC25 CP $02 Jump to Response_PleaseBeMoreSpecific if more than one key is present.
EC27 JP NC,Response_PleaseBeMoreSpecific
EC2A LD HL,$E51D HL=Data_ItemGroup_Key.
EC2D CALL CheckActiveScenicEvents Call CheckActiveScenicEvents.
EC30 CP $28 Jump to Take_JewelledKey if the key is "A jewelled key.".
EC32 JP Z,Take_JewelledKey
EC35 CP $47 Jump to Take_GoldKey if the key is "A gold key.".
EC37 JP Z,Take_GoldKey
Continue on to Take_CopperKey.
