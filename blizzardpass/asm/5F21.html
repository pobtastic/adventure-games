5F21: Obey Command
Used by the routine at GameLoop.
Turns the verb and noun into a movement or a scripted action. PUSH, PULL, ROLL or MOVE with CURTAINS is rewritten to noun 2B. WET, COOL, DAMP or SOAK is refused, except with the robe, the door or the running water.
Curtains are a special noun for the pushing verbs.
