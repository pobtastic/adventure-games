72D5: Copy Origin Attributes
Used by the routine at SetOrigin.
Copies attribute rows from SourceOrigin into DestinationOrigin until *DestinationOrigin_Height equals the stop patched by SetOrigin. SetOrigin and ResetOrigin also patch the width copied on each row.
CopyOrigin_Attributes 72D5 LD BC,($7337) Fetch *SourceOrigin.
72D9 CALL AttributeRow Call AttributeRow.
72DC PUSH HL Stash the source pointer on the stack.
72DD LD BC,($7339) Fetch *DestinationOrigin.
72E1 CALL AttributeRow Call AttributeRow.
72E4 POP DE Copy the source pointer into DE (using the stack).
The width immediate is self-modified by SetOrigin and ResetOrigin.
72E5 LD BC,$0000 Load that width.
72E8 EX DE,HL Point HL at the source and DE at the destination.
72E9 LDIR Copy one attribute row.
Step to the next row.
72EB LD A,($7338) Increment *SourceOrigin_Row.
72EE INC A
72EF LD ($7338),A
72F2 LD A,($733A) Decrement *DestinationOrigin_Height.
72F5 DEC A
72F6 LD ($733A),A
The stop immediate is self-modified by SetOrigin_Copy.
72F9 CP $00 Jump to CopyOrigin_Attributes until *DestinationOrigin_Height equals that stop.
72FB JR NZ,CopyOrigin_Attributes
72FD RET Return.
