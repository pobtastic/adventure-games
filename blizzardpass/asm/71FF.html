71FF: Copy Parameter Block
Used by the routines at PictureAddress and PaintShape.
Copies a length-prefixed parameter block from the shape stream to Parameter_Count and terminates it with FB.
CopyParameterBlock 71FF INC HL Step to the length.
7200 LD C,(HL) Fetch it into BC.
7201 LD B,$00
7203 LD DE,$5B9E Point DE at Parameter_Count.
7206 INC HL Step past the length.
7207 LDIR Copy those bytes.
7209 LD ($5BCC),HL Store HL in Saved_Shape_Pointer.
720C LD HL,$5B9F Point HL at Parameter_Byte.
720F LD A,$FB Terminate that copy with FB.
7211 LD (DE),A
7212 RET Return.
