63D4: Print System Message
Used by the routines at GameLoop, RefuseCommand, DescribeLocation, ListCarried, OfferAnotherGame, PressAnyKey, GetObject and RestoreGame.
Prints system message A from SystemMessages. The messages are the game's stock replies: what you can see, what you are carrying, and why a command failed.
PrintSystemMessage 63D4 LD B,A Jump to PrintNumberedMessage with the message number in B.
63D5 JR PrintNumberedMessage
