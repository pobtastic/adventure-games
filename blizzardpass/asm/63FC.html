63FC: Plot Character
Used by the routines at ObeyCommand, DescribeLocation, PrintCharacter and PrintFlag.
Puts A on the screen. 0C rubs out the previous character. 0D moves to the next row, pausing and scrolling when the text hits the bottom of the window.
DELETE is printed as a space and then another DELETE, so the cursor steps back.
