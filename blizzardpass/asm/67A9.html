67A9: Condition: Flag Is Not Zero
Passes when the flag byte selected by the parameter is not zero.
FlagNotZero 67A9 CALL FlagAddress Call FlagAddress to point at the flag.
67AC LD A,(HL) Pass when the flag is not zero.
67AD OR A
67AE RET NZ
67AF JR RejectCondition Jump to RejectCondition to reject this bytecode condition.
