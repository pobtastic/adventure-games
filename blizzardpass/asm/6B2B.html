6B2B: Finish Word On Terminator
Used by the routine at Capitalise.
A space that follows a message terminator is forced, so the terminator itself is not printed in the middle of a word.
FinishWord 6B2B LD A,($5B9B) A last character of 7E is a terminator.
6B2E CP $7E
6B30 JR Z,FinishWord_Space
6B32 CP $5E A last character of 5E is a terminator. Anything else is collected as a space.
6B34 LD A,$20
6B36 JP NZ,CollectWord
FinishWord_Space 6B39 LD A,$20 A terminator is replaced by a space as the last character.
6B3B LD ($5B9B),A
6B3E JP CollectWord Jump to CollectWord to collect that space.
