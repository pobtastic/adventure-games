696C: Action: Room Changed
Used by the routines at MoveToRoom, MoveToObject, GetObject, DropObject, Saved, RemoveObject, SwapObjects, SetObjectLocation, DropEverything and RedrawLocation.
Sets the room-changed flag and walks the per-room records at RoomRecords so an illustration that depended on an object is redrawn. Verbs 43, 8A, 8B, 85 and 8E skip that walk.
