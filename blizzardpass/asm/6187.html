6187: Describe Location
Used by the routines at GameLoop, ObeyCommand, OfferAnotherGame and RedrawLocation.
Prints where the player is, what is here, and which ways lead out. Bank 6 supplies the long text for rooms below 66, and it opens "welcome to Blizzard Pass by Alan Cox. This program is dedicated to Hawkwind. Any resemblance between the characters in the game and your mother in law is total coincidence". A dark room says so and lists nothing. The text is underlined before the cursor is put back for the prompt.
Remember the cursor, and choose a row. Pictures use row 0D so the text sits under the illustration.
