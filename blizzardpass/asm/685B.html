685B: Action: Play Again
Prints message 12, the game is over, and asks Y or N. Y starts again at GameLoop. N resets the machine.
OfferAnotherGame 685B CALL DescribeLocation Call DescribeLocation to describe the ending.
685E XOR A The picture may be drawn again.
685F LD ($6185),A
6862 CALL DrawRoomOnce Call DrawRoomOnce to draw it.
6865 LD A,$12 Message 12 asks whether to play again.
6867 CALL PrintSystemMessage
OfferAnotherGame_Key 686A CALL WaitKeyDown Wait for a key.
686D CP $4E N (4E) resets the Spectrum.
686F JR Z,ResetMachine
6871 CP $59 Any key but Y (59) asks again.
6873 JR NZ,OfferAnotherGame_Key
6875 JP GameLoop Jump to GameLoop to start another game.
