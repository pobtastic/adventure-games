66F9: Condition: Room Above Parameter
Passes when the current room is higher than the parameter in B.
RoomAbove 66F9 LD A,($5D87) Compare the room with the parameter.
66FC CP B Jump to RejectCondition to reject this bytecode condition.
66FD JR Z,RejectCondition
66FF JR C,RejectCondition
6701 RET Return.
