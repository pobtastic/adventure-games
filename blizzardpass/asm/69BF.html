69BF: Action: Swap Objects
Exchanges the locations of the object in the parameter and the object addressed by A, then marks the room changed.
SwapObjects 69BF CALL ObjectAddress Point at the first object's location.
69C2 PUSH HL
69C3 LD B,A Point at the second object.
69C4 CALL ObjectAddress
69C7 LD A,(HL) Exchange the two location bytes.
69C8 LD (HL),C
69C9 POP HL
69CA LD (HL),A
69CB JR RoomHasChanged Jump to RoomHasChanged once the two locations have been exchanged.
