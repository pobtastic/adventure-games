73D2: Plot Shape With 08
Used by the routine at DispatchShape.
Opcode F6 plots the following shape after writing 08 to ShapeSuppressed. No instruction reads that byte, and no picture record uses this opcode.
Input
IX Picture record
OpcodeF6 73D2 LD B,(IX+$01) Fetch the following shape.
73D5 LD A,$08 Load 08 to store at ShapeSuppressed.
73D7 INC IX Advance the record pointer.
73D9 JP DispatchShape_Flag Jump to DispatchShape_Flag.
