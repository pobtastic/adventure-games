62D7: Expand Dictionary Word
Used by the routine at FindMessage.
Treats A as an index into Dictionary. Word 0 is empty. Every other word ends with bit 7 set.
ExpandToken 62D7 LD HL,$B1BC Token 0 is the first dictionary word.
62DA LD B,A
62DB OR A
62DC JR Z,ExpandToken_Character
62DE DEC HL The scan starts on the byte before the dictionary.
ExpandToken_Scan 62DF INC HL Skip to the end of one word. Bit 7 marks it.
62E0 BIT 7,(HL)
62E2 JR NZ,ExpandToken_Next

ExpandToken_Next 62E6 DJNZ ExpandToken_Scan Count down to the requested word.
