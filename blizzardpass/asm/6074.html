6074: Run Script Table
Used by the routine at ObeyCommand.
Walks a bytecode table. A line whose verb and noun match is executed; a failed condition or a bit-6 action stops the line. 7F ends the table.
The ordinary command scripts live at CommandScripts and are marked by a clear script-set flag.
