67C6: Condition: Flag Not Below Parameter
Passes when the selected flag is not below A. The failing jump at 67CB is also the first word of the condition table, so condition 0 does not have a routine of its own.
The remaining condition routines are 66EB, 66F2, 66F9, 6702, 6709, 6713, 671A, 6726, 6739, 674E, 6740, 6747, 672D, 6733, 67A1, 67A9, 6759, 6760, 6767, 6777, 67C6, 67BF, 67B1, 67B8 and 6716.
FlagNotBelow 67C6 CALL FlagAddress Call FlagAddress to address the flag.
67C9 CP (HL)
67CA RET NC
67CB JR RejectCondition Jump to RejectCondition to reject this bytecode condition.
