612B: Step Past Condition
Used by the routines at RunScripts and SkipLine.
Moves HL past a condition and its parameter. Condition numbers below 15 have no extra object byte.
SkipCondition 612B LD A,(HL) Step past the condition byte and its object byte.
612C INC HL
612D INC HL
612E CP $15
6130 RET C Conditions below 15 have no further parameter.
6131 INC HL Step past the parameter byte.
6132 RET Return.
