655A: Key Repeat Timer
How long to wait before scrolling, measured against the ROM frame counter. The cursor keys store 0000, 0190 or 00C8 here.
RepeatTimer 655A DEFW $00C8 Delay.
655C DEFW $0000 Rows already scrolled.
