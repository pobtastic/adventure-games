62F2: Capitalise After A Full Stop
Used by the routine at ExpandToken.
After a full stop, a letter is forced to capitals. A space is handled by FinishWord so a terminator cannot split a word.
Capitalise 62F2 PUSH BC Keep the character while the last one is inspected.
62F3 LD B,A
62F4 LD A,($5B9B) A preceding full stop capitalises the next letter.
