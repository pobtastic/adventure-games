C000: Unused Bank 7
Not the picture bytecode, and not the bank paged at C000 in the resting snapshot or during play. That window is bank 0: port 7FFD holds 10, and the game's saved page value at 66D7 is 10. The page routine at 66BD ORs 10 and writes the port, and it is only ever asked for banks 0, 3 and 6. The BASIC loader does OUT 32765,23 so that LOAD runs with this bank visible, but the copier then writes the adventure into banks 6, 3 and 0 and pages bank 0 back. Nothing is copied here, so the body is zero.
