C104: Keep Token
Used by the routine at LookupWord.
The first word below id 55 is the verb. IT and THEM substitute the previous noun. POLISH, SKIN and BRIDGE are rewritten to internal verbs 2C, 2B and 31. Later words are the noun, then extra words.
Input
A Token id
HL Line pointer
Count this word. A third word is stored as an extra, not as the noun.
