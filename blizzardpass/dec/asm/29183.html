29183: Copy Parameter Block
Used by the routines at PictureAddress and PaintShape.
Copies a length-prefixed parameter block from the shape stream to Parameter_Count and terminates it with 251.
CopyParameterBlock 29183 INC HL Step to the length.
29184 LD C,(HL) Fetch it into BC.
29185 LD B,0
29187 LD DE,23454 Point DE at Parameter_Count.
29190 INC HL Step past the length.
29191 LDIR Copy those bytes.
29193 LD (23500),HL Store HL in Saved_Shape_Pointer.
29196 LD HL,23455 Point HL at Parameter_Byte.
29199 LD A,251 Terminate that copy with 251.
29201 LD (DE),A
29202 RET Return.
