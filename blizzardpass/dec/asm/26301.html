26301: Page RAM Bank
Used by the routines at DescribeLocation and WaitForKey.
Selects RAM bank A at 49152 and forces the 48K ROM, which is the ROM this game's calls expect. A is the bank in the low three bits. Callers pass 0, 3 or 6. Bank 7, which the loader paged with OUT 32765,23, is never requested again.
PageBank 26301 PUSH BC Save what the caller passed in, since BC is needed for the memory paging port 32765.
26302 LD BC,32765
26305 OR 16 Force the 48K ROM and write the bank in the low three bits.
26307 OUT (C),A
26309 POP BC Restore BC from the stack.
26310 RET Return.
