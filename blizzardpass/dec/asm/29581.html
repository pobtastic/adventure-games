29581: Reset Drawing Origin
Used by the routine at DispatchShape.
Opcode 243 sets *SourceOrigin, *DestinationOrigin and *SourceOrigin_Row to 0, *DestinationOrigin_Height to the bottom illustration row 11, and the copy width to 32. It then copies that full-width rectangle through SetOrigin_Copy. The destination row counts down, so the copy is upside down.
