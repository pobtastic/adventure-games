29650: Plot Shape With 8
Used by the routine at DispatchShape.
Opcode 246 plots the following shape after writing 8 to ShapeSuppressed. No instruction reads that byte, and no picture record uses this opcode.
Input
IX Picture record
OpcodeF6 29650 LD B,(IX+1) Fetch the following shape.
29653 LD A,8 Load 8 to store at ShapeSuppressed.
29655 INC IX Advance the record pointer.
29657 JP DispatchShape_Flag Jump to DispatchShape_Flag.
