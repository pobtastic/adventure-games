29835: Mirror Pixel Row
Used by the routine at VerticalRun_Patch.
Reverses eight pixel rows of the current character row about its midpoint. VerticalRun_Draw patches the span and the inner pair count; VerticalRun_Patch and FixedVertical patch the outer loop stop.

C Column
