AB0B: Event: Clear The Flag
Clears B700, the flag that makes S type an F as well. The record at CAB5 calls this.
Event_ClearTheFlag AB0B SUB A Clear the flag at B700.
AB0C LD ($B700),A Update *B700.
AB0F RET Return.
