82A5: Command: PRINT
Turns the printer on when bit 6 of port FB is clear. Command_PRINT_NOPRINT is NOPRINT, which turns it off. Reached through the special-key table at 8271, dispatched from Command_MatchASpecialKey.
Command_PRINT 82A5 IN A,($FB) Read the printer port FB.
82A7 BIT 6,A Test bit 6.
82A9 JR NZ,CommandDispatchAControl Jump to CommandDispatchAControl if bit 6 is set.
82AB LD A,$01 Take 01.
82AD JR Command_PRINT_0 Jump to Command_PRINT_0 to store the flag.
Reached through the special-key table at 8271, dispatched from Command_MatchASpecialKey.
Command_PRINT_NOPRINT 82AF XOR A Clear the accumulator.
Command_PRINT_0 82B0 LD ($B6F2),A Write it to the printer-on flag at B6F2.
Continue on to CommandDispatchAControl.
