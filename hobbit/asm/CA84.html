CA84: Table: Object Handlers
Records of 07 bytes: an id, a flag, a handler, another flag and a second handler. FF at CACA ends the list. The BF bytes from here to CB42 take in this table and the Special Object Records at CACB, each with its terminator. 6C1F copies them to 5F1D and 6C5D copies them back for a new game, and 8471, 8500 and 853C load, save and verify them. 96CB walks this table. Read by Start, LoadGame, SaveGame and TurnAdvanceTheOtherCharacters.
