70DC: Stored Registers For PrintMessage
Six bytes. PrintMessage saves A, DE and IX here, and PrintMessage_TokenByNibble_Return puts them back.
PrintMessage_SavedA 70DC DEFB $00 A saved by PrintMessage and restored by 7363.
PrintMessage_SavedDE 70DD DEFW $0000 DE saved by PrintMessage and restored by PrintMessage_TokenByNibble_Return.
70DF DEFB $00 Byte tested by 745C while a class token is printed.
