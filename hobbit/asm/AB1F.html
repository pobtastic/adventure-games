AB1F: Event: Drops From Above
Prints the forest warning and can sting the player. The forest record at CAC1 calls this.

AB22 CALL PrintMessage Call PrintMessage to print message.
AB25 LD A,($C12B) Return if the current location is the one saved at B6F3.

AB29 LD HL,$B6F3
