782B: Inglish: Follow The Grammar Flag
Used by the routine at InglishRunTheGrammar.
Follows the grammar flag in E. Bit 6 copies four grammar bytes, bit 7 copies ten, and neither asks "what?" and reports no match.
Inglish_FollowTheGrammarFlag 782B BIT 6,E Jump to Inglish_CopyFourGrammarBytes if bit 6 of the grammar flag is set.
782D JR NZ,Inglish_CopyFourGrammarBytes
782F BIT 7,E Jump to Inglish_CopyTenGrammarBytes if bit 7 of the grammar flag is set.
7831 JR NZ,Inglish_CopyTenGrammarBytes
7833 CALL Inglish_AskWhat Call Inglish_AskWhat to ask "what ?" when a conjunction is still open.
7836 XOR A Return zero.
7837 RET Return.
