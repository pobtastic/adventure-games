90D2: Action: You Are Dead
Used by the routines at Action_Direction, Action_Examine, Turn_ThePlayerHasDied, Event_TheBarrel, Event_TheDeepBog, Event_ARandomHalf, Event_TheWebSmothersYou, Event_StungFromAbove and EventForestriver.
Kills the player and ends the game. Reached through the action list of $00 - "You" for action 00, dispatched by TriggerAction.
YouAreDead 90D2 SUB A Clear the character whose turn it is.
90D3 LD ($B6EA),A Update *CurrentCharacter.
90D6 LD HL,$AFF1 Point at the message "You are dead" (AFF1).
90D9 CALL PrintMessage Call PrintMessage to print message.
90DC CALL GameOver_Start Call GameOver_Start to print the score.
This entry point is used by the routine at Event_LocationTwentyFive.
This is the same key wait as Normal_AnyKey, but it ends with a jump to Restart to restart the game.
YouAreDead_WaitForKey 90DF XOR A Read from the keyboard port.
90E0 IN A,($FE)
90E2 AND %00011111 Keep bits 0-4, one for each key in the half-row. XOR A then IN A,(FE) reads every half-row at once, and a pressed key reads as a reset bit.
90E4 CP $1F Loop back to YouAreDead_WaitForKey until any key has been pressed.
90E6 JR Z,YouAreDead_WaitForKey
90E8 JP Restart Jump back to Restart to restart the game.
