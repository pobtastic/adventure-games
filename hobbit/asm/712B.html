712B: Inglish: Describe The Action
Used by the routines at Inglish_ArmTheMessageGate, InglishApplyAMatch, Turn_ApplyTheSavedMatch and ResponseThatWillNotWork.
Prints the sentence for the current action: who, the verb, and the objects. Bit 4 of the packed token classes (B71D) suppresses the whole sentence. What the other bits select is only partly confirmed.
Decide whether a sentence is due, and which noun it names.
