A448: Action: Goblin Capture
Runs for the goblins $3D - "Nasty Goblin", $45 - "Hideous Goblin" and $49 - "Horrible Goblin" to $4C - "Disgusting Goblin". Their action lists reach here through Turn_RunTheHandlerIfPresent_Examine_CallHandler and TriggerAction, called from Turn_RunTheHandlerIfPresent. B holds the character from CurrentCharacter when reached through Turn_ApplyTheSavedMatch_ReadTheCurrentCharacter, and 00 when reached through 7B3A. The player's location is compared with it before the goblin speaks. Reached through the action lists of $3D - "Nasty Goblin", $45 - "Hideous Goblin", $49 - "Horrible Goblin", $4A - "Mean Goblin", $4B - "Vicious Goblin" and $4C - "Disgusting Goblin" for action 00, dispatched by TriggerAction.
