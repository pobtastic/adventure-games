AAD5: Event: Close The Magic Door
Closes the magic door. The magic door record at CAA9 calls this.
Event_CloseTheMagicDoor AAD5 LD HL,$C388 Point HL at C388.
AAD8 RES 5,(HL) Close that object.
AADA LD HL,$B03F Point at the message "the magic door close(s|d|ing|es).[0x14]" (B03F).
AADD JP Event_TheLakeOrTheRiver Jump to Event_TheLakeOrTheRiver to print it at 1E or 1C.
