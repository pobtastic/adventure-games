812B: Drawing: Advance The Low Coordinate
Used by the routines at DrawingSetup and Drawing_PlotTheSelectedPixel.
Advances the low coordinate. If that sets bit 7, the coordinate is put back and the routine returns zero.
Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate 812B INC E Step the low coordinate on and jump to Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate_StoreTheCoordinate if bit 7 is clear.
812C BIT 7,E
812E JR Z,Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate_StoreTheCoordinate
8130 DEC E Put the low coordinate back.
8131 LD H,A Keep A in H.
8132 XOR A Set the zero flag.
8133 LD A,H Bring A back from H.
8134 RET Return.
This entry point is used by the routine at Drawing_RetreatTheLowCoordinate.
Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate_StoreTheCoordinate 8135 LD H,A Keep A in H.
8136 OR $01 Clear the zero flag.
8138 LD A,H Bring A back from H.
8139 RET Return.
