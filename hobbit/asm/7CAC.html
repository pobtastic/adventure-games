7CAC: Inglish: Copy One Clause Pair
Used by the routine at Inglish_SaveTheClauseWindow.
Copies one id-and-location pair from the object into the second match buffer, and stops when 06 or the clause bits say so.
Inglish_CopyOneClausePair 7CAC XOR A Return when the count in B is zero.
7CAD CP B
7CAE RET Z
7CAF LD D,$00 The clause offset's high byte is zero.
