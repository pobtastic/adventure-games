AA91: Event: A Hole Appears
Makes $0B - "Mountains Side Door" visible, then at location 2A prints that a hole appears. The hole record at CAC8 calls this.
Event_AHoleAppears AA91 LD HL,$C2BC Make $0B - "Mountains Side Door" visible, and return if the current location is not 2A.
AA94 SET 7,(HL)
AA96 LD A,($C12B)
AA99 CP $2A
AA9B RET NZ
AA9C LD HL,$B277 Point HL at B277.
AA9F JP PrintMessage Jump back to PrintMessage to print "there is a loud crack and a hole appears".
