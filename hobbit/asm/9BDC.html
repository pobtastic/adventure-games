9BDC: Object: Move Contents
Moves everything inside the object in A (recursively) to the location in B; if the player is among them, the player moves too. The byte here is set while the player has not been found among those contents.
Object_MoveContents 9BDC DEFB $FF
This entry point is used by the routines at Action_Direction, Action_Give, Action_Examine, Action_Unlock, ActionCapture, Event_TheBarrel, Event_TheDeepBog, Event_ARandomHalf and Event_GiveTheLunch.
