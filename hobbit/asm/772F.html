772F: Inglish: Clear The Match Bit
Clears the bit that says a class matched. Reached through the jump table at JumpTable, dispatched from 75D0.
InglishClearTheMatchBit 772F RES 0,E Clear the flags so bit 0 is off.
This entry point is used by the routines at InglishRejectAClass and InglishCheckTheWordClass.
InglishClearTheMatchBit_0 7731 LD D,$30 Class 30 selects the match-bit test.
