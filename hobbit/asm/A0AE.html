A0AE: Exits: Point At The List
Used by the routines at MessageListTheObjects and DisplayExits.
Finds the current location and points IX at its exit list, seven bytes into the record. BC is 03, the length of one exit.
Find the location's record.
ExitsPointAtTheList A0AE CALL LocateLocation Call LocateLocation to locate location.
A0B1 LD BC,$0007 Step IX on by 0007 bytes, to the exit list.
A0B4 ADD IX,BC
A0B6 LD BC,$0003 One exit is 0003 bytes.
A0B9 RET Return.
