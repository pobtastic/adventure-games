71D5: Table: Address Past The Location
Used by the routines at Message_PrintTheSecondNoun and Inglish_CopyANameIntoTheClause.
Finds the location record and returns the address of its name. The name token starts two bytes in.
Table_AddressPastTheLocation 71D5 PUSH IX Find the location record.
71D7 CALL LocateLocation
71DA PUSH IX Take that record's address.
71DC POP HL
71DD INC HL Step to the location's name, two bytes into the record.
71DE INC HL
71DF POP IX Restore IX from the stack.
71E1 RET Return.
