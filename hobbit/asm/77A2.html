77A2: Inglish: Walk The Grammar
Walks one step of the grammar, including groups in brackets. Reached through the jump table at JumpTable, dispatched from 75D0.
InglishWalkTheGrammar 77A2 CALL Inglish_CountAnotherGrammarTry Call Inglish_CountAnotherGrammarTry to count another grammar try.
InglishWalkTheGrammar_Loop 77A5 CALL Inglish_ReadTheNextGrammarByte Call Inglish_ReadTheNextGrammarByte to read the next grammar byte.
77A8 CP $70 Jump back to InglishWalkTheGrammar if the grammar byte is 70.
77AA JR Z,InglishWalkTheGrammar
77AC CP $80 Jump to Inglish_KeepTheUnknownWord unless the grammar byte is 80.

77B0 BIT 4,E Call Inglish_AskWhat if bit 4 of the grammar flag is clear.
