AAB3: Event: Open The Magic Door
Opens the magic door and an elf sweeps past. The magic door record at CAAC calls this.
Event_OpenTheMagicDoor AAB3 LD HL,$C388 Point HL at C388.
AAB6 SET 5,(HL) Mark that object open.
AAB8 LD HL,$B037 Point at the message "the magic door open(s|d|ing|es).[0x14]" (B037).
AABB CALL Event_TheLakeOrTheRiver Call Event_TheLakeOrTheRiver to print the lake or the river.
AABE LD HL,$B113 Point HL at "an elf sweeps past".
AAC1 CALL Event_TheLakeOrTheRiver Call Event_TheLakeOrTheRiver to print the lake or the river.
AAC4 JP Event_TheRing Jump to Event_TheRing to handle the ring.
