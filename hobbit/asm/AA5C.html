AA5C: Event: The Spider
Clears bits on the spider and stores the token SPIDER. The spider record at CA8D calls this.
Event_TheSpider AA5C LD IX,$C205 Point IX at $07 - "Spider Web".
AA60 RES 3,(IX+$07) Mark the spider as not broken (clear bit 3).
AA64 RES 5,(IX+$07) Close the object.
AA68 SLA (IX+$05) Double the strength and keep the overflow.
AA6C LD DE,$0623 Store the token SPIDER.
AA6F LD ($C20F),DE Store DE at C20F.
AA73 RET Return.
