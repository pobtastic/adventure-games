A0C8: Exits: To The North There Is
Used by the routine at YouSeeStart.
Walks the location's exit triples. For each exit guarded by a visible object it prints "to the <direction> there is". Direction 09 is printed as ABOVE and direction 0A as BELOW.
Point at this location's exits.
