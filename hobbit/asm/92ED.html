92ED: Action: Use The Other Object
Used by the routines at Action_Shoot and Event_TheBarrel.
Uses the second object on the current one. A fluid, a broken object, or a combat byte of 83 will not work. Reached through the action lists of $01 - "Heavy Rock Door", $05 - "Round Green Door", $07 - "Spider Web", $0A - "Goblins Back Door", $0C - "Large Trap Door", $0E - "Short Strong Sword", $13 - "Barrel", $19 - "Bow", $1A - "Strong Arrow", $1E - "Trap Door", $21 - "Large Cupboard", $25 - "Wooden Chest" and $29 - "Wooden Boat" for action 0B, dispatched by TriggerAction.
