8271: Table: Special Keys
Thirteen token pairs, then the handler word for each pair. Command_MatchASpecialKey scans the pairs and jumps 1A bytes on to the handler. The first pair is empty. The rest are ALL, EXCEPT, IT, ONE, PRINT, NOPRINT, LOAD, SAVE, QUIT, HELP, SCORE and PAUSE.
