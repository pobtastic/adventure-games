A4C0: Action: Open The Goblins Door
Opens $11 - "Goblins Door" when the character in *Pointer_CurrentCharacter is in 10. The action list stores this address at C357, and the copy of the objects stores it at F63C. Reached through the action list of $11 - "Goblins Door" for action 10, dispatched by TriggerAction.
Action_OpenTheGoblinsDoor A4C0 LD IX,($B70C) Point IX at the character in *Pointer_CurrentCharacter.
A4C4 LD A,(IX+$10) Read the location from the object record.
A4C7 CP $10 Jump back to ResponseThatWillNotWork if the object's location is not 10.
A4C9 JP NZ,ResponseThatWillNotWork
A4CC CALL ActionOpenOrClose Call ActionOpenOrClose to open or close the object.
A4CF CALL Turn_GateTheMessage Call Turn_GateTheMessage to let the message through or hold it back.
A4D2 LD A,($CA99) Copy the goblins door id at CA99 into the flag at CA9A.
A4D5 LD ($CA9A),A
A4D8 RET Return.
