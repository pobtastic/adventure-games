92B5: Action: Add To The Player's Strength
Used by the routines at Action_RollAgainstTen and Event_ARandomHalf.
Adds 0A to the player's strength. At 80 the player reads that foul gluttony has killed you. Reached through the action lists of $00 - "You", $22 - "Food" and $26 - "Lunch" for action 1B, dispatched by TriggerAction.
If the message is held back, the player's strength is not increased.
