77B9: Inglish: Keep The Unknown Word
Used by the routines at InglishWalkTheGrammar and InglishStepOverAGroup.
Keeps the unknown word at B6DA as the token pointer while the matcher counts at 60 and 50 are still live.
Inglish_KeepTheUnknownWord 77B9 CP $60 Jump to InglishStepOverAGroup if the grammar byte is 60.
77BB JR Z,InglishStepOverAGroup
77BD CP $50 Jump to InglishRunTheGrammar if the grammar byte is 50.
77BF JR Z,InglishRunTheGrammar
77C1 LD HL,($B6DA) Read *B6DA.
77C4 LD ($B6DC),HL Update *B6DC.
77C7 JR InglishRunTheGrammar_0 Jump to InglishRunTheGrammar_0 to keep a count of 50.
