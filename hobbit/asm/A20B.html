A20B: Table: Verb List
Five action ids. A1D4 compares the current action with these bytes, and ExitsTheDirectionWord reads A20E as the direction-word table. Read by Action_MatchAVerbInTheTable.
A20B DEFB $0E,$11,$12 0E drop, 11 put in, 12 put on.
A20E DEFB $14,$2C 14 take out of, 2C throw. ExitsTheDirectionWord adds twice the direction id to this address.
