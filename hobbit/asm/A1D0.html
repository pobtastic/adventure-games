A1D0: Action: Match A Verb In The Table
Used by the routines at Turn_RunTheActionHandler and Turn_RunTheHandlerIfPresent.
Matches the current action against the five verb bytes at A20B and returns when one of them matches.
Action_MatchAVerbInTheTable A1D0 PUSH HL Count 05 passes.
A1D1 PUSH BC
A1D2 LD B,$05
A1D4 LD HL,$A20B Point HL at table: Verb List.
A1D7 LD A,($B6E7) Read *CurrentAction.

A1DB JR Z,Action_MatchAVerbInTheTable_ReturnFromTheVerbTable
A1DD INC HL Step to the next byte in display exits.
A1DE DJNZ Action_MatchAVerbInTheTable_TestTheVerb Loop back to Action_MatchAVerbInTheTable_TestTheVerb until 05 runs out.

A1E1 POP HL
A1E2 RET Return.
