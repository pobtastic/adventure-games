70F3: Clause: Pack The Action Scores
Used by the routines at Inglish_DescribeTheAction, Inglish_BuildTheActionSentence and Inglish_ScoreTheSwappedClause.
Packs two scores from the action entry into the clause. The high nibbles of bytes 5 and 7 are stored at B71E. The high nibbles of bytes 1 and 3 are stored at B71D.

70FD LD A,(IX+$07) Read byte 7 of this action entry.

7102 ADD A,C

7106 LD A,(IX+$01) Read byte 1 of this action entry.

7116 LD ($B71D),A Update *B71D.
7119 RET Return.
