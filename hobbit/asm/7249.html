7249: Input: Wait For A Key
Used by the routine at ReadTheInputLine.
Counts down the input delay while polling the keyboard. A key raises the next delay by 01F4, up to 0BB8, and is returned. If the delay runs out, 7291 is printed into the buffer, ENTER is returned, and the next delay is cleared.
