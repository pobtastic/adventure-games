8ED2: Action: Locate And Follow
Used by the routine at Action_CarryOnWithTheObject.
Finds where the object is. An open container is followed to the object inside, and that location is returned.
Action_LocateAndFollow 8ED2 PUSH IX Call LocateObject to locate the object.
