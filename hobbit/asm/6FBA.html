6FBA: Input: Compare The Dictionary Word
Used by the routine at Input_TakeTheNextWord.
Compares the typed word with the dictionary word of the same length.
Input_CompareTheDictionaryWord 6FBA LD A,($708A) Read *708A.
6FBD LD B,A Copy the result into B.
6FBE LD A,($709B) Read *709B.
6FC1 CP B Jump to Input_CompareTheDictionaryWord_PointAtTheTypedWord if the length of the dictionary word is not below the length of the typed word.
6FC2 JR NC,Input_CompareTheDictionaryWord_PointAtTheTypedWord
6FC4 LD B,A Copy the result into B.
Input_CompareTheDictionaryWord_PointAtTheTypedWord 6FC5 LD HL,$707A Point HL at the typed word.
6FC8 LD DE,$708B Point DE at the dictionary word.
Input_CompareTheDictionaryWord_CompareTheLetters 6FCB LD A,(DE) Return if the dictionary word is not the typed word.
6FCC CP (HL)
6FCD RET NZ
6FCE INC DE Step to the next byte of the dictionary word.
6FCF INC HL Step to the next byte of the typed word.
6FD0 DJNZ Input_CompareTheDictionaryWord_CompareTheLetters Loop back to Input_CompareTheDictionaryWord_CompareTheLetters until the length of the dictionary word runs out.
6FD2 RET Return.
