813A: Drawing: Retreat The Low Coordinate
Used by the routines at DrawingSetup and Drawing_PlotTheSelectedPixel.
Retreats the low coordinate. If that sets bit 7, the coordinate is put back and one step is counted.
Drawing_RetreatTheLowCoordinate 813A DEC E Step the low coordinate back and jump to Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate_StoreTheCoordinate if bit 7 is clear.
813B BIT 7,E
813D JR Z,Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate_StoreTheCoordinate
813F INC E Put the low coordinate back.
8140 RET Return.
