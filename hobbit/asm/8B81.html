8B81: Keyboard Row Masks
One mask byte per keyboard half-row, the half-row and key bits of the last newly pressed key in the word at 8B89, and the previous scan at 8B8B. GetKey compares each new row with that scan.
KeyboardRowMasks 8B81 DEFB $01,$00,$00,$0D,$02,$00,$00,$02
8B89 DEFW $0000
8B8B DEFB $00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00
