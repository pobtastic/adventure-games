A210: Table: Direction Words
NORTH through DOWN. ExitsTheDirectionWord reads the direction token at A20E plus twice the direction id, so direction 01 is the first word here.

A212 DEFW $0607 SOUTH

A216 DEFW $0779 WEST
A218 DEFW $0485 NORTHEAST
A21A DEFW $048E NORTHWEST

A220 DEFW $0725 UP
A222 DEFW $01C4 DOWN
