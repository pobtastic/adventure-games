842E: Print: Percentage Digit
Used by the routine at GameOver_Start.
Turns one decimal digit of the score into ASCII, counting up from '0'.
Converts e.g. "0075" into "7.5" in ASCII for display purposes.
PercentageAscii 842E LD A,$2F Start the quotient digit one below '0'.
PercentageAscii_Loop 8430 INC A Count the digit up by one.
8431 AND A Clear carry for the subtraction.
8432 SBC HL,DE Subtract the power of ten in DE from HL.
8434 JR NC,PercentageAscii_Loop Jump back to PercentageAscii_Loop to count another digit unless the subtraction went below zero.
8436 ADD HL,DE Add the power of ten back to leave the remainder.
8437 CP $30 Compare against 30 (ASCII "0") to set flags for the return.
8439 RET Return.
