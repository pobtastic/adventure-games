78B7: Inglish: Copy Two Bytes From The Other Clause
Used by the routine at InglishMatchOneWord.
Copies byte 0 and byte 1 from the other clause record into this clause. When bit 7 of the grammar flag is set, the name bytes are copied too.
Inglish_CopyTwoBytesFromTheOtherClause 78B7 EXX Switch to the saved registers for this phrase.
78B8 LD A,(IX+$01) Read byte 1 of the other clause record.
78BB AND $7F OR it, with bit 7 clear, into the clause byte at offset 1.
78BD OR (IY+$01)
78C0 LD (IY+$01),A Update the clause byte at offset 1.
78C3 LD A,(IX+$00) Read byte 0 of the other clause record.
78C6 LD (IY+$00),A Update the clause byte at offset 0.
78C9 EXX Switch to the saved registers for this phrase.
78CA LD A,(IY+$1C) Read byte +1C from IY.
78CD OR (IY+$1D) Return if bytes 1C and 1D are both zero.
