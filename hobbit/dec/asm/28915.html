28915: Clause: Pack The Action Scores
Used by the routines at Inglish_DescribeTheAction, Inglish_BuildTheActionSentence and Inglish_ScoreTheSwappedClause.
Packs two scores from the action entry into the clause. The high nibbles of bytes 5 and 7 are stored at 46878. The high nibbles of bytes 1 and 3 are stored at 46877.

28918 RRCA Take the high nibble of byte 5.

28925 LD A,(IX+7) Read byte 7 of this action entry.

28930 ADD A,C

28934 LD A,(IX+1) Read byte 1 of this action entry.
