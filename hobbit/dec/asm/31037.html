31037: Grammar Workspace
Slots the grammar fills while a clause is matched. Inglish_ClearTheMatchBuffer clears the first 17 bytes. Nothing executes these bytes. Read by Inglish_ClearTheMatchBuffer, Inglish_ScoreTheCurrentAction, Inglish_ResolveTheFirstNoun, Inglish_NoteTheFirstNoun, Inglish_NoteTheSecondNoun, Inglish_TestTheScratchBits, Inglish_BuildTheActionSentence, Inglish_SaveTheClauseWindow, Inglish_ScoreTheSwappedClause, Inglish_FindTheFirstNoun, Inglish_RunTheFirstNounTest, Inglish_FindTheSecondNoun, Inglish_RunTheSecondNounTest, Inglish_CopyTheParserSlot and Inglish_PlantJumpRelativeIfNonZero. Read by Inglish_ScoreTheSwappedClause, Inglish_RunTheFirstNounTest and Inglish_CopyTheParserSlot.
