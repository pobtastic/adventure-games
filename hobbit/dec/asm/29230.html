29230: Inglish: Compare One Clause Pair
Used by the routine at Inglish_MatchClausePairs.
Compares one pair of clause bytes with the pair the caller addressed. An empty pair matches. Otherwise the low nibbles of the second bytes must agree and the first bytes must be equal. Both pointers then move on by two.
Inglish_CompareOneClausePair 29230 PUSH HL Jump to Inglish_CompareOneClausePair_Advance if this pair is empty.
29231 LD A,(HL)

29233 OR (HL)

29239 XOR (HL) Jump to Inglish_CompareOneClausePair_Advance if its low nibble differs from the clause byte.
29240 AND 15
