35713: Keyboard Row Masks
One mask byte per keyboard half-row, the half-row and key bits of the last newly pressed key in the word at 35721, and the previous scan at 35723. GetKey compares each new row with that scan.
KeyboardRowMasks 35713 DEFB 1,0,0,13,2,0,0,2
35721 DEFW 0
35723 DEFB 0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0
