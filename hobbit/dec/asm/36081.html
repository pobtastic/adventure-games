36081: Action: Add The Object's Mass
Used by the routines at Action_Pickup and Action_ThrowAt.
Adds the current object's mass to what the character is carrying. Too much and the player sees "You are carrying too much".

36091 ADD A,(IX+3) Add this object's mass onto the count.
36094 JR NC,Action_AddTheObjectMass_Putdown_CopyTheResult Jump to Action_AddTheObjectMass_Putdown_CopyTheResult if the sum did not overflow.
36096 LD A,255 Cap the total mass at 255.
Action_AddTheObjectMass_Putdown_CopyTheResult 36098 LD B,A Copy the result into B.
36099 LD IY,(46860) Read *Pointer_CurrentCharacter.
