30800: Inglish: Copy Four Grammar Bytes
Used by the routines at InglishRunTheGrammar, Inglish_FollowTheGrammarFlag and Command_FollowTheGrammarFlag.
Clears bit 6 of the grammar flag and copies four bytes into the record, then joins the ten-byte copy.
Inglish_CopyFourGrammarBytes 30800 RES 6,E Clear the flags so bit 6 is off.
30802 PUSH DE The copy starts 4 bytes into the record.
30803 LD DE,4
30806 JR Inglish_CopyTenGrammarBytes_PointAtTheClause Jump back to Inglish_CopyTenGrammarBytes_PointAtTheClause to copy the ten grammar bytes.
