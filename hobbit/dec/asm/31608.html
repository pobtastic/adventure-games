31608: Inglish: Split The Clause Flags
Used by the routines at Inglish_ScoreTheCurrentAction and Inglish_BuildTheActionSentence.
Splits the clause flags into the noun-print flag, the second-noun flag and the bytes kept for the match.

31611 AND 64 Keep only bit 6.
31613 LD (46865),A Store the result at 46865.
31616 LD A,(46877) Read *46877.
31619 LD B,A Copy the result into B.
31620 AND 1 Keep only bit 0.

31626 AND 128 Keep only bit 7.
31628 JR Z,Inglish_SplitTheClauseFlags_StoreTheNounPrintFlag Jump to Inglish_SplitTheClauseFlags_StoreTheNounPrintFlag if bit 7 of the phrase scratch byte is clear.
