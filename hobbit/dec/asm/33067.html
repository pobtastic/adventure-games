33067: Drawing: Advance The Low Coordinate
Used by the routines at DrawingSetup and Drawing_PlotTheSelectedPixel.
Advances the low coordinate. If that sets bit 7, the coordinate is put back and the routine returns zero.
Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate 33067 INC E Step the low coordinate on and jump to Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate_StoreTheCoordinate if bit 7 is clear.
33068 BIT 7,E
33070 JR Z,Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate_StoreTheCoordinate
33072 DEC E Put the low coordinate back.
33073 LD H,A Keep A in H.
33074 XOR A Set the zero flag.
33075 LD A,H Bring A back from H.
33076 RET Return.
