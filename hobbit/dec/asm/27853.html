27853: Squiggle Line
This section draws the "Squiggle" line which separates the windows.
SquiggleLine 27853 CALL ClearScreen Call ClearScreen to clear screen.
27856 LD HL,20800 Point at the squiggle row on screen.
27859 LD DE,28108 Point at the squiggle pattern.
27862 LD C,5 Five rows of the squiggle are drawn.
SquiggleLine_OuterLoop 27864 LD B,16 Set a counter of 16.
27866 PUSH HL
SquiggleLine_InnerLoop 27867 LD A,(DE) Read the next byte of the picture.
27868 LD (HL),A Store it at 20800.
27869 INC HL Increase both pointers by one.
27870 INC DE
27871 LD A,(DE) Store the squiggle pattern in the squiggle row.
27872 LD (HL),A
27873 INC HL Step HL on.
27874 DEC DE Step back one byte through Graphics_Squiggle.
27875 DJNZ SquiggleLine_InnerLoop Decrease counter by one and loop back to SquiggleLine_InnerLoop until counter is zero.
27877 INC DE Step on through Graphics_Squiggle.
27878 INC DE
27879 POP HL Move to the next screen row and repeat while squiggle rows remain.
27880 INC H
27881 DEC C
27882 JR NZ,SquiggleLine_OuterLoop
Continue on to TurnReadAndTokeniseTheCommand.
