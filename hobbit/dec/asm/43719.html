43719: Event: The Lake Or The River
Used by the routines at Event_OpenTheMagicDoor and Event_CloseTheMagicDoor.
Prints the lake or the river message when the current location is 30 or 28. Any other location returns at once.
Test the flag.
Event_TheLakeOrTheRiver 43719 LD A,(49451) Jump to PrintMessage if *49451 is 30.
43722 CP 30

43729 JP Z,PrintMessage
