29257: Input: Wait For A Key
Used by the routine at ReadTheInputLine.
Counts down the input delay while polling the keyboard. A key raises the next delay by 500, up to 3000, and is returned. If the delay runs out, 29329 is printed into the buffer, ENTER is returned, and the next delay is cleared.
