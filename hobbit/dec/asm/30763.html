30763: Inglish: Follow The Grammar Flag
Used by the routine at InglishRunTheGrammar.
Follows the grammar flag in E. Bit 6 copies four grammar bytes, bit 7 copies ten, and neither asks "what?" and reports no match.
Inglish_FollowTheGrammarFlag 30763 BIT 6,E Jump to Inglish_CopyFourGrammarBytes if bit 6 of the grammar flag is set.
30765 JR NZ,Inglish_CopyFourGrammarBytes
30767 BIT 7,E Jump to Inglish_CopyTenGrammarBytes if bit 7 of the grammar flag is set.
30769 JR NZ,Inglish_CopyTenGrammarBytes
30771 CALL Inglish_AskWhat Call Inglish_AskWhat to ask "what ?" when a conjunction is still open.
30774 XOR A Return zero.
