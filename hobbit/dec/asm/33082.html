33082: Drawing: Retreat The Low Coordinate
Used by the routines at DrawingSetup and Drawing_PlotTheSelectedPixel.
Retreats the low coordinate. If that sets bit 7, the coordinate is put back and one step is counted.
Drawing_RetreatTheLowCoordinate 33082 DEC E Step the low coordinate back and jump to Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate_StoreTheCoordinate if bit 7 is clear.
33083 BIT 7,E
33085 JR Z,Drawing_AdvanceTheLowCoordinate_StoreTheCoordinate
33087 INC E Put the low coordinate back.
33088 RET Return.
