32442: Inglish: Fill The Claimed Slots
Used by the routine at Action_TalkTo.
Gives the current object up to A of the claimed character slots at 46904: it writes the object id into the first A slots marked 255 (claimed by Command_FollowTheGrammarFlag_ClaimTheSlot), capping A at the claimed-slot count in 46903, then frees the rest of the claimed slots by writing 0 to them.
