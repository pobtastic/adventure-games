51844: Table: Object Handlers
Records of 7 bytes: an id, a flag, a handler, another flag and a second handler. 255 at 51914 ends the list. The 191 bytes from here to 52034 take in this table and the Special Object Records at 51915, each with its terminator. 27679 copies them to 24349 and 27741 copies them back for a new game, and 33905, 34048 and 34108 load, save and verify them. 38603 walks this table. Read by Start, LoadGame, SaveGame and TurnAdvanceTheOtherCharacters.
