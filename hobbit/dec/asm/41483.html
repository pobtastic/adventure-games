41483: Table: Verb List
Five action ids. 41428 compares the current action with these bytes, and ExitsTheDirectionWord reads 41486 as the direction-word table. Read by Action_MatchAVerbInTheTable.
41483 DEFB 14,17,18 14 drop, 17 put in, 18 put on.
41486 DEFB 20,44 20 take out of, 44 throw. ExitsTheDirectionWord adds twice the direction id to this address.
