AACC: Action: Add To Score
Adds the operand to the score at Score.
When the condition succeeded, add the operand to the score.
Action_AddToScore AACC JP NZ,Handler_RunScript_10 Leave the score unchanged.
AACF LD A,(IX+$01) The operand is the points to add.
AAD2 CALL AddToScore Call AddToScore.
AAD5 JP Handler_RunScript_10 Resume the script.
