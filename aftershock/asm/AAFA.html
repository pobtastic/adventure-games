AAFA: Action: Increment Inventory
Increases Count_InventoryItems by one.
When the condition succeeded, the player has gained an item.
Action_IncrementInventory AAFA JP NZ,Handler_RunScript_11 Add one to the inventory count at Count_InventoryItems.
AAFD LD HL,$A0D2
AB00 INC (HL)
AB01 JP Handler_RunScript_11
