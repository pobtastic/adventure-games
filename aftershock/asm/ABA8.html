ABA8: Action: List Inventory
Prints the inventory, or the empty-inventory message.
When the condition succeeded, list what the player is carrying.
Action_ListInventory ABA8 JP NZ,Handler_RunScript_11 Leave the inventory unlisted.
ABAB CALL Action_Inventory Call Action_Inventory.
ABAE JP Handler_RunScript_11 Resume the script.
