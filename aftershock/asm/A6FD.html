A6FD: Script Condition State
Working bytes for Handler_RunScript. The mask collects condition results and the operator selects AND or OR.
ScriptConditionMask A6FD DEFB $40 Condition mask.
ScriptConditionOperator A6FE DEFB $FF FF selects AND and FE selects OR.
