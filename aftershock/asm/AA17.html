AA17: Action: Print Message And Newline
Prints the compressed message addressed by the operand, then a newline.
When the condition succeeded, print the message addressed by the operand and start a new line.
Action_PrintMessageAndNewline AA17 JP NZ,Handler_RunScript_9 Leave the message unprinted.
AA1A LD L,(IX+$01) The message addressed by the operand.
AA1D LD H,(IX+$02)
AA20 CALL PrintCompressedStringAndNewline Call PrintCompressedStringAndNewline.
