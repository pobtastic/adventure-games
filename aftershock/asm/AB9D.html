AB9D: Action: Describe Room
Prints the current room, including its picture when the room has one.
When the condition succeeded, describe the current room and force its picture.
Action_DescribeRoom AB9D JP NZ,Handler_RunScript_11 Leave the room undescribed.
ABA0 LD E,$01 Force its picture.
ABA2 CALL Handler_RoomExits Call Handler_RoomExits.
ABA5 JP Handler_RunScript_11 Resume the script.
