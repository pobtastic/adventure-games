A0A8: Flags: Already Seen Room Images
One bit for each entry in Table_RoomsWithImages. Handler_RoomExits sets the bit after the picture has been shown so the same room does not draw it again.
Flags_SeenRoomImages A0A8 DEFB $01 Seen-image bits 00 to 07.
A0A9 DEFB $00 Seen-image bits 08 to 0F.
A0AA DEFB $00 Seen-image bits 10 to 17.
