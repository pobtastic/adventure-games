A97F: Condition: Room Pair Matches
Succeeds when CurrentRoom is the first operand and TemporaryRoom is the second.
Output
F The zero flag is set when both rooms match
Succeed only when the player is in the first room and the marked room is the second.
Condition_RoomPairMatches A97F LD A,($A106) The player is in the first room.
A982 CP (IX+$01)
A985 JR NZ,RoomPairMatches_Done
A987 LD A,($AD78) The marked room is the second.
A98A CP (IX+$02)
RoomPairMatches_Done A98D JP Handler_RunScript_2 Resume the script.
