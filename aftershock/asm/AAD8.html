AAD8: Action: Pause
Waits for the operand number of interrupts.
When the condition succeeded, wait for the operand number of frames.
Action_Pause AAD8 JP NZ,Handler_RunScript_10 Do not wait.
AADB LD B,(IX+$01) The operand is how many frames to wait.
AADE CALL Pause_Loop Call Pause_Loop.
AAE1 JP Handler_RunScript_10 Resume the script.
