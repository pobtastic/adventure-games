AE2C: Handler: Can Take Item
Used by the routines at Condition_CanTakeItem and Condition_CannotTakeItem.
Rejects a take when the item is already carried or the inventory is full.
Plural items in Table_PluralItems use the "them" message.
Input
A Item ID
Output
A 00 when the item can be taken, otherwise 01
F The zero flag is set when the item can be taken
