EC5F: Events: Wolves
Wolves attack. The bow drives them off; otherwise the player dies.
Print " A pack of wolves appears.".
Events_Wolves EC5F LD HL,$DA61 HL=Messaging_APackOfWolvesAppears.

EC6A JR NZ,Events_Wolves_Next Jump to Events_Wolves_Next if "A bow" isn't held.
EC6C LD A,$04 Call IsObjectCarried with item 04: "Some arrows".
EC6E CALL IsObjectCarried
EC71 JR Z,Events_Wolves_FightOff Jump to Events_Wolves_FightOff if "Some arrows" is held.
