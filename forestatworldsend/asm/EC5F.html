EC5F: Events: Wolves
Wolves attack. The bow drives them off; otherwise the player dies.

EC62 CALL PrintStringAndANewline

EC6A JR NZ,Events_Wolves_Next

EC6E CALL IsObjectCarried
EC71 JR Z,Events_Wolves_FightOff
Events_Wolves_Next EC73 LD HL,$DA7C Print "You have nothing to fight with. They pounce upon you.".
