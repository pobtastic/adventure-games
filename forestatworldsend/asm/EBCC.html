EBCC: Events: Flying Lizard
A flying lizard attacks. Sometimes the blow is fatal.
Events_FlyingLizard EBCC LD HL,$D7D1 Print " A Flying Lizard attacks you.".
EBCF CALL PrintStringAndANewline
EBD2 LD A,$0F Jump to Events_FlyingLizard_Next unless a roll of 0F comes up zero.
EBD4 CALL GenerateRandomNumber
EBD7 JR NZ,Events_FlyingLizard_Next
EBD9 LD HL,$EB23 Make the blow fatal and print "You move too slowly. It's jaws crush you.".
EBDC EX (SP),HL
EBDD LD HL,$D823
EBE0 JR Events_FlyingLizard_Print
Events_FlyingLizard_Next EBE2 LD HL,$D7EF The blow only glances, with "But you deftly avoid the the vicious,snapping jaws.".
Events_FlyingLizard_Print EBE5 CALL PausePrintStringAndScroll Call PausePrintStringAndScroll to pause, then print that message and start a new line.
EBE8 RET Return.
