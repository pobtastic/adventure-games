ECB6: Handler: Injury Check
Used by the routines at MovePlayer, Action_Climb, Action_Enter and GameStart.
Refuse the move while the player is badly hurt. Otherwise change rooms and arm the local dangers.
Handler_InjuryCheck ECB6 LD C,A C=A.
ECB7 LD A,$48 Call ValidateItemPresent with item 48: "You are seriously injured".
ECB9 CALL ValidateItemPresent
ECBC JR NZ,Handler_InjuryCheck_Next Jump to Handler_InjuryCheck_Next if "You are seriously injured" is not in the current room.
Print "You can't move. You are too badly injured.".
ECBE LD HL,$DB38 HL=Messaging_YouCantMove.
ECC1 CALL PrintStringAndNewline Call PrintStringAndNewline.
ECC4 RET Return.
