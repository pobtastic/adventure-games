F8AB: Action: Row
Row the boat, if the player is in it and has the oars.
Action_Row F8AB CALL Parser_ValidateDirectObject Return if the words after the verb are not valid for this command.
F8AE RET C
F8AF LD HL,$E925 Jump to Action_Row_YouCant unless the player named crossing the river.
F8B2 CALL Parser_AdvanceToSeparator
F8B5 JR NZ,Action_Row_YouCant
F8B7 LD A,($BA9B) Jump to Action_Row_Next when the player is in room 1D (In the Boat).
F8BA CP $1D
F8BC JR Z,Action_Row_Next
Reply with "You're not in the boat.".
F8BE LD HL,$DE33 Print "You're not in the boat." and return to the game loop.
F8C1 JP PrintResponse
Action_Row_Next F8C4 LD A,$1D Call GetObjectLocation to fetch where the boat is.
F8C6 CALL GetObjectLocation
F8C9 CP $1A Jump to Action_Row_WesternBank unless the boat is in room 1A (The Western Bank of a Broad River).
F8CB JR NZ,Action_Row_WesternBank
F8CD LD BC,$1D1B Put object 1D (the boat) in room 1B (The Eastern Bank of a Broad River).
F8D0 CALL UpdateObjectLocation
F8D3 JR Action_Row_Across Jump to Action_Row_Across to say the boat has crossed.
Action_Row_WesternBank F8D5 LD BC,$1D1A Put object 1D (the boat) in room 1A (The Western Bank of a Broad River).
F8D8 CALL UpdateObjectLocation
Reply with "You row across to the opposite bank.".
Action_Row_Across F8DB LD HL,$E34A Print "You row across to the opposite bank." and return to the game loop.
F8DE JP PrintResponse
Action_Row_YouCant F8E1 JP Response_YouCant_GameLoop Tell the player the command is impossible.
