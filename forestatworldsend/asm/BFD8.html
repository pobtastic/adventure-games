BFD8: Parser: Match Input Word
Split the command into words and look each one up in the vocabulary.
Blank the four-character word buffer.
Parser_MatchInputWord BFD8 LD DE,$BB41 Start at the word buffer (BB41).
BFDB LD B,$04 Prepare a space and a counter of 04 characters.
BFDD LD A,$20
MatchInputWord_Clear BFDF LD (DE),A Write spaces through the word buffer.
BFE0 INC DE
BFE1 DJNZ MatchInputWord_Clear
BFE3 XOR A Jump to Handler_UnknownWord when no token slots remain, or the command has ended.
BFE4 OR C
BFE5 JP Z,Handler_UnknownWord
BFE8 LD A,(HL)
BFE9 CP $0D
BFEB JP Z,Handler_UnknownWord
Copy the next word, up to 04 characters, stopping at a space, a comma or ENTER.
BFEE LD B,$04 Copy up to 04 characters into the word buffer.
BFF0 LD DE,$BB41
MatchInputWord_Copy BFF3 LD A,(HL) Jump to MatchInputWord_IgnoreThe when the character is ENTER.
BFF4 CP $0D
BFF6 JR Z,MatchInputWord_IgnoreThe
BFF8 CP $20 Jump to MatchInputWord_Delimiter when the character is a space or a comma.
BFFA JR Z,MatchInputWord_Delimiter
BFFC CP $2C
BFFE JR Z,MatchInputWord_Delimiter
C000 LD (DE),A Copy the character into the word buffer and advance both pointers.
C001 INC HL
C002 INC DE
C003 DJNZ MatchInputWord_Copy Repeat from MatchInputWord_Copy until four characters have been copied.
A longer word is skipped up to the next delimiter.
MatchInputWord_Skip C005 LD A,(HL) Jump to MatchInputWord_IgnoreThe on ENTER, or to MatchInputWord_Delimiter on a space or a comma.
C006 CP $0D
C008 JR Z,MatchInputWord_IgnoreThe
C00A CP $20
C00C JR Z,MatchInputWord_Delimiter
C00E CP $2C
C010 JR Z,MatchInputWord_Delimiter
C012 INC HL Skip this character and keep looking for a delimiter.
C013 JR MatchInputWord_Skip
MatchInputWord_Delimiter C015 INC HL Skip consecutive spaces and commas.
C016 LD A,(HL)
C017 CP $20
C019 JR Z,MatchInputWord_Delimiter
C01B CP $2C
C01D JR Z,MatchInputWord_Delimiter
Ignore the word "THE ".
MatchInputWord_IgnoreThe C01F EXX Switch in the spare registers and point them at "THE " and the word.
C020 LD HL,$BB5C
C023 LD DE,$BB41
C026 LD B,$04
MatchInputWord_The C028 LD A,(DE) Compare the four characters.
C029 CP (HL)
C02A JR NZ,MatchInputWord_AfterThe
C02C INC DE
C02D INC HL
C02E DJNZ MatchInputWord_The
MatchInputWord_AfterThe C030 EXX Switch back to the main registers to act on the comparison.
C031 JR Z,Parser_MatchInputWord Jump back to Parser_MatchInputWord when the word was THE, so it is not tokenised.
C033 DEC C Use up one token slot.
C034 EXX

C042 LD B,$04

C045 CP (HL)
C046 JR NZ,MatchInputWord_NextWord Jump to MatchInputWord_NextWord when it does not match.

C049 INC HL
C04A DJNZ MatchInputWord_CompareChar
