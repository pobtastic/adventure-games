Overview
Rescue princess Mara from Zarn's dungeon and bring her back to the Great Valley.
Primary walkthrough: Jacob Gunness via the Classic Adventures Solution Archive (file id 285), with August 2010 bug fixes by Jim Grimwood. Dorothy Millard's Spectrum walkthrough (Tipshop / her homepage) is a useful cross-check for optional farm food/coin and HELP/KISS variants at the nymph.
  • Random attackers may appear - KILL them WITH BOW (or WITH SWORD later)
  • Save often; combat can miss

Random Encounters
Gunness: characters appear at random and may kill you - KILL creature WITH BOW, and save often. Enemies listed in the route may also show in adjacent locations.
Code inspection of the random helper: elf and flying lizard scenic hits commonly use a 1/15 fatal roll per appearance; Korl warrior 1/20; many bow attacks miss on a 1/5 roll and must be retried. Not playthrough-verified.
Timed Threats
Spider, slime monster, demons and Zarn arm a short countdown on entry (six turns in this binary). Clear them before it expires: KILL SPIDER WITH BOW, KILL MONSTER WITH BOW, KILL DEMONS WITH SWORD, KILL WIZARD/ZARN WITH SWORD. After the wolves injure you, wait/LOOK until the wood nymph heals you before moving on.
Optional Items And Variants
Gunness skips farmhouse food and the woodman's coin. Millard collects both; they are optional on this route. YS tips say kiss the nymph; Gunness uses LOOK until healed, moves south, then GET RING. TOUCH ROCK and RUB ROCK are treated as equivalent in tips. Tipshop's older Gunness mirror inserts an extra S, W before the return BLOW HORN; the CASA file (with Grimwood fixes) does not - this page follows CASA.
