The Story
You set out to rescue Princess Mara, who has been captured by the wizard Zam. The game was written by David M. and Jean A. Banner with an inlay by Terry Greer, and cost £5.50. Sources: reviews, Spectrum Computing.
You Fire An Arrow
Crash pointed out that shooting the elf prints "You fire an arrow:-", yet the parser doesn't know the word FIRE. The message is at Messaging_YouFireAnArrow, and it is printed by Handler_AnnounceArrow. Crash also missed a SCORE command and found that going east and then west doesn't always bring you back where you started. See the reviews.
Four Words For The Chasm
Personal Computer News noted that crossing the chasm needs a four-word command with a preposition, and Personal Computer Games finished the game in about three hours, despite flying lizards that kill at random. See the reviews.
Reviews
Crash issue 10 gave it 6/10, Big K issue 9 2/3 and Personal Computer Games issue 15 5/10. It was later on the Your Sinclair issue 48 Smash Tape Special Edition. Source: Spectrum Computing.
Unused Messages
Three short response messages, at Messaging_FirstUnusedResponseMessage, Messaging_SecondUnusedResponseMessage and Messaging_ThirdUnusedResponseMessage, are never printed.
