5CFC: Custom Load Routine
Used by the routine at BootstrapEntry.
Custom edge sampler used by the bootstrap. It still calls the ROM edge routines at 05E3 / 05E7, but with Micromega timing thresholds rather than the standard ROM pilot. On error it restores the border from BORDCR and prints "ATTEMPTED BREAK!" or "TAPE LOADING ERROR".
