5CCB: BASIC Bootstrap
The short BASIC program "KENTILLA" that starts the Micromega tape. It autostarts at line 1. The REM on line 1 holds the bootstrap at Load_Stage_Two, the loader at Load_Tape_Block, a header workspace at Stage2Header and the loader messages. Line 2 is POKE 0,0 (an intentional ROM no-op). Line 3 sets black INK/PAPER/BORDER, CLS, CLEARs just below the loader (MicromegaLoader) and enters it with RANDOMIZE USR Load_Stage_Two. Line 4 is the ordinary ROM LOAD "" CODE that later replaces the stage-2 loader with the final support block at SupportCode.
BasicBootstrap 5CCB DEFB $00,$01,$3A,$01,$EA Line 1 header and REM token.
