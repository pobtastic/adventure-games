5CD0: Bootstrap Entry Point
Stage-1 loader kept inside the line-1 REM. It reads a 17-byte custom header into Stage2Header, pulls the stage-2 length and address from that header, loads 0258 bytes to E9B6, then jumps there.
BootstrapEntry 5CD0 LD IX,$5D96
5CD4 LD DE,$0011
5CD7 XOR A
5CD8 SCF
5CD9 CALL CustomLoad
5CDC JP NC,CustomLoad_12
5CDF LD A,($5C47)
5CE2 CP $06
5CE4 JP NZ,$0000
5CE7 LD DE,($5D96)
5CEB LD IX,($5D98)
5CEF LD A,$FF
5CF1 SCF
5CF2 CALL CustomLoad
5CF5 JP NC,CustomLoad_12
5CF8 EI
5CF9 JP MicromegaLoader
