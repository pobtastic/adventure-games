Spectrum Computing
Kentilla (Derek Brewster, Micromega 1984; Mastertronic 1986 reissue).
Reviewed With His Own Ratings
Derek Brewster wrote CRASH's Adventure Trail column and rated each adventure under five headings. When Your Spectrum reviewed his own adventure Kentilla, it marked the game the same way; one of its three reviewers gave it 2 out of 5. Source: code198x.com.
