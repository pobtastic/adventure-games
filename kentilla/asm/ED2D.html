ED2D: Search Vocabulary (Verbs)
Used by the routine at JumpTable.
Jump-table target JumpTable_SearchVerbs (USR JumpTable_SearchVerbs): search Table_Verbs for the token at InputBuffer_TokenStart. Sets ParserState_MatchLength to 02 and walks up to index 2E. On a hit, returns BC=word index for BASIC GO TO n*100+1000 (see JumpTable); on a miss, BC=0000.
