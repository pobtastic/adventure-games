F0D3: Scroll Screen
Used by the routines at JumpTable and SubmitInputLine.
Jump-table target JumpTable_ScrollAdjust. Raises the line byte of S_POSN until it is at least 05. Each step calls CL_SCROLL.
ScrollAdjust F0D3 LD A,($5C89) Return if the line byte of S_POSN is at least 05.
F0D6 CP $05
F0D8 RET NC
F0D9 INC A Increment the line byte of S_POSN.
F0DA LD ($5C89),A
F0DD LD B,$0F Call CL_SCROLL 0F times.
ScrollAdjust_0 F0DF CALL $0E00
F0E2 DJNZ ScrollAdjust_0
F0E4 JR ScrollAdjust Jump to ScrollAdjust.
