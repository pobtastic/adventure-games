EFFB: Draw Location Graphic
Used by the routine at JumpTable.
Jump-table target JumpTable_LocationGraphicSetup. Points CHARS at F128, draws the script for *LocationId, then restores the ROM font at 3C00.

EFFD LD ($5BF6),A
F000 LD HL,$F128 Write F128 to *CHARS.

F006 CALL LocationGraphicSetup_SelectScript Call LocationGraphicSetup_SelectScript.

F00C LD ($5C36),HL
F00F RET Return.
