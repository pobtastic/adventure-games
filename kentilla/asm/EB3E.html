EB3E: Input Line Symbol-Shift Remap
Used by the routine at InputLine.
Reached from InputLine_SymbolShiftHeld when Symbol Shift is held. Remaps P to " and J to "-", then continues into InputLine_HandleKey; any other key returns to the poll loop at InputLine_Poll.
InputLine_SymbolShiftRemap EB3E LD A,E Jump to InputLine_SymbolShiftRemap_J unless the key is "P".
EB3F CP $50
EB41 JR NZ,InputLine_SymbolShiftRemap_J
EB43 LD A,$22 Remap it to " and handle it at InputLine_HandleKey.
EB45 LD E,A
EB46 JR InputLine_HandleKey
InputLine_SymbolShiftRemap_J EB48 CP $4A Jump to InputLine_Poll unless the key is "J".
EB4A JR NZ,InputLine_Poll
EB4C LD A,$2D Remap it to "-" and handle it at InputLine_HandleKey.
EB4E LD E,A
EB4F JR InputLine_HandleKey
