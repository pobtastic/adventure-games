FFFB: ROM Return Stub
Two DEC SP instructions and a jump to ROM STMT_RET. The Mastertronic reissue POKEs the same five bytes before its ROM loads; on this Micromega image they arrive with the final support block.
RomReturnStub FFFB DEC SP Decrease SP by two.
FFFC DEC SP
FFFD JP $1B7D Jump to STMT_RET.
