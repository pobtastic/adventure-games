23980: Restore Border
Success return from Load_Tape_Block. Restores the border from BORDCR, then either returns with the load flags intact or prints "ATTEMPTED BREAK!" and hangs. Load_Tape_Block_PrintError also enters the print loop at Restore_Border_PrintLoop for "TAPE LOADING ERROR".
