23958: Stage-2 Header
Seventeen bytes read from the tape's special header for the stage-2 loader. After a successful header read this holds the length 0600 and destination MicromegaLoader.
Stage2Header 23958 DEFB 88,2 Stage-2 length.
Fetched into IX by Load_Stage_Two after the header load.
Stage_Two_Destination 23960 DEFB 182,233 Stage-2 destination.
23962 DEFB 32,75,78,32,76,79,32,88 Name and copy of the length/destination fields.
23970 DEFB 2,182,233,0
23974 DEFB 128 End marker.
