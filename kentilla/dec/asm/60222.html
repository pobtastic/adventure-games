60222: Input Line Symbol-Shift Remap
Used by the routine at InputLine.
Reached from InputLine_SymbolShiftHeld when Symbol Shift is held. Remaps P to " and J to "-", then continues into InputLine_HandleKey; any other key returns to the poll loop at InputLine_Poll.
InputLine_SymbolShiftRemap 60222 LD A,E Jump to InputLine_SymbolShiftRemap_J unless the key is "P".
60223 CP 80
60225 JR NZ,InputLine_SymbolShiftRemap_J
60227 LD A,34 Remap it to " and handle it at InputLine_HandleKey.
60229 LD E,A
60230 JR InputLine_HandleKey
InputLine_SymbolShiftRemap_J 60232 CP 74 Jump to InputLine_Poll unless the key is "J".
60234 JR NZ,InputLine_Poll
60236 LD A,45 Remap it to "-" and handle it at InputLine_HandleKey.
60238 LD E,A
60239 JR InputLine_HandleKey
